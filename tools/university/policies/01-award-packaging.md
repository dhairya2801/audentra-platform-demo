## Scope and authority
These are fictional Aster evaluation parameters, not representations of federal law or actual award-year limits. They supplement the institutional corpus in the v3 world only. Aster's authoritative sources are the approved policy version for the student's effective date and scope, followed by the student's approved exception. An informal communication cannot amend a policy or approve an exception.

## Award ceilings and periods
Annual ceilings in this fixture are: Pell $7,395; Aster Merit $12,000; Aster Access $16,000; Aster Global $20,000; Campus Employment authorization $3,000. Fall and Spring installments together cannot exceed the accepted annual award. International students cannot receive Pell or Aster Access. Campus employment is permission to earn wages: it is never an account credit or a promised payment. Income/dependency modeling and actual federal calculations are outside this fixture.

## Posting and balances
Annual tuition, mandatory fees, housing and meals are divided equally between Fall and Spring. The $500 enrollment deposit is an advance payment credited against tuition, not an extra charge. Books and personal expenses belong to cost of attendance, not the student-account ledger. A submitted or failed transfer is not a credit. A reversal retains the original posting and adds an equal opposite posting.

Fall aid is scheduled for 4 September, Spring for 22 January. Verification holds delay aid, not course registration. Accepted award amounts are promises conditional on eligibility; only posted disbursements reduce the balance. Refunds increase a negative ledger balance toward zero; they must never create a debt. Full-time aid uses 12 credits at census. A proposed drop requires a new aid review; Edward must not invent an exact award reduction.
