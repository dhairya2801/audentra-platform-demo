A case has one accountable owner and ordered prerequisites. Offices may complete their own ready steps; downstream work waits for evidence. A case closes only when every required step has completed or been explicitly waived with a reason. Delivered advice is not a completed outcome. Bounced messages do not satisfy notification steps.

For a verification-to-registration case: Financial Aid verifies the document; Financial Aid authorizes the award posting; Student Accounts reconciles the posted balance; Student Accounts reviews the financial hold; Enrollment Services confirms the student received the outcome. Satisfying verification alone cannot mark the case resolved.

For accessible housing: Accessibility Services certifies the functional accommodation (no diagnosis exposed); Housing identifies an available compatible bed; Housing offers and confirms placement. Certification does not reserve a bed. No other office may override an accessibility decision.

An adviser on leave retains ownership; the named covering adviser provides service until leave ends. A parent with revoked or expired authorization receives no student-specific billing information, even if their identity is known. Consent has subject, scope, valid window and revocation time.

Facts have effective time and recorded time. A correction received late may change what is now known about a prior term, but must not appear in an answer about what the institution knew before receipt. Proactive contact must use current ownership, eligibility and delivery evidence, and must avoid duplicate reminders for the same open case.
