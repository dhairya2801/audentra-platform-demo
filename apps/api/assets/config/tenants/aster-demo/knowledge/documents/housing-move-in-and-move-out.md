## Fall 2026 move-in

- **New residents: Saturday 22 August 10:00 – Sunday 23 August 18:00.**
  Each student receives an arrival slot (a two-hour window by hall and floor)
  by portal message on 17 August; slots can be swapped in the portal.
- **Returning residents: Saturday 29 August 10:00–18:00.**
- Students **may move in before orientation begins** — the move-in weekend
  precedes Orientation Sessions A–C (24–26 August); there is no need to wait
  for orientation.
- International students attending International Check-In (21–22 August)
  may arrive on **Thursday 20 August** at no charge; request the early
  arrival on the Housing page.
- Other **early arrivals** (athletics, employment, a documented travel
  constraint) are requested on the Housing page by 10 August and cost **$30
  a night** with approval; halls do not open before 18 August.

At check-in the resident presents photo ID, receives the room key and card
access, and completes the room condition report in the portal within 48
hours; damage not reported then is charged at move-out.

Spring 2027 move-in for new residents: Saturday 16 – Sunday 17 January, 10:00–18:00.

## Move-out

- **Winter break:** halls close **Saturday 19 December 2026 at 12:00**;
  residents leave within 24 hours of their last exam unless approved for
  break housing (see *Break and guest policy*).
- **End of year:** halls close **Saturday 8 May 2027 at 12:00**; graduating
  seniors may stay until Commencement (15 May) on request.
- Check-out: clear and clean the room, return the key to the front desk, and
  complete the check-out form; a $75 improper check-out fee applies when the
  key is not returned.

## Storage and shipping

Aster provides no summer storage. Packages may be shipped to the hall's mail
room (Cedarcroft Commons for all halls) from 15 August, addressed with the
student's name and hall.
