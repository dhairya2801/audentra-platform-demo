## Direct Loans

Federal Direct Loans are offered from the FAFSA to eligible domestic
students. **Subsidised** loans are need-based and accrue no interest while
the student is enrolled at least half-time; **unsubsidised** loans are not
need-based and accrue interest from disbursement. Annual limits for a
dependent first-year student are $5,500 (at most $3,500 subsidised), rising
in later years; the award letter shows the amount offered.

Before a first Direct Loan disburses the student completes, once, at the
federal student-aid site:

1. the **Master Promissory Note**, and
2. **entrance counselling**.

Both show as conditions on the Aid page until Financial Aid receives
confirmation (usually the next business day). Loans disburse in two equal
halves, Fall and Spring, on the disbursement dates; **first-year, first-time
borrowers receive the Fall half 30 days after the first day of classes (30
September 2026)**, as federal rules require. A borrower may reduce or cancel
a loan within 14 days of a disbursement by asking Financial Aid.

Repayment begins six months after the student drops below half-time
enrollment; exit counselling is required before leaving Aster.

## Federal Work-Study

Work-Study is a need-based allocation, not a grant: the student earns it by
working an on-campus job (or an approved community-service placement) and is
paid **biweekly wages**, which are not credited to the bill. Jobs are listed
by the Career Center (Innovation Hall 120) from the first day of classes;
the student applies, is hired by the department, and completes payroll
paperwork with the department before the first shift. Limits: **20 hours a
week** during term, 35 during breaks; earnings stop when the allocation is
reached. An allocation not used by the end of Spring is forfeited.

International students are not eligible for Work-Study but may hold
on-campus jobs under the *International student employment* policy.
