## Summer 2027

Summer session runs **Mon 24 May – Fri 30 July 2027**. Summer courses are
billed per credit and are not covered by the Aster Merit or Aster Access
scholarships. Fall 2027 classes begin Mon 30 August 2027; Fall 2027
registration for continuing students opens 5 April 2027 and add/drop is 10
September 2027.

## Reading the calendar

Deadlines end at 23:59 Eastern on the date shown. When a deadline falls on a
day offices are closed it does not move; portal submissions are accepted
until 23:59. Each entry's owning office is the place to ask: registration and
academic dates — Registrar; billing dates — Student Accounts; aid dates —
Financial Aid; housing dates — Housing & Residence Life; orientation — New
Student Programs; international check-in — International Student Services.
