## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering (**Department of
Engineering**, **128 credits**) follows the common Engineering first year
and branches in the second year into circuits, digital systems and signals.
STEM-designated.

| Course | Credits | Recommended term | Prerequisites |

Grade of C or better in every engineering, mathematics and physics course.

## First-year plan

Fall: ENGR 101, CS 101, MATH 151, PHYS 151, WRIT 101 (17 credits). Spring:
EE 220, MATH 152, PHYS 152, AST 100, one humanities course (15 credits).

## Entry

As for Mechanical Engineering: direct admission, or internal transfer with
MATH 151 and PHYS 151 (C or better) and a 2.5 GPA.
