## Evaluation run

After each Fall and Spring grade posting (22 December 2026; 11 May 2027) the
Disbursement & SAP Specialist runs the evaluation: GPA (2.00), pace (67%),
maximum timeframe (150% of the program's credits — 180 for a 120-credit
program, up to 195 for the 130-credit BSN). Statuses post to the aid record
within two business days and every *warning* and *not meeting* student is
messaged with the figures and the appeal route; not-meeting students are
also flagged to their financial-aid counselor and adviser for outreach
within five business days.

## Appeals

Received on the portal form; complete when the statement, documentation and
an **academic plan** signed by the adviser are attached. Incomplete appeals
are returned within two business days with what is missing.

The committee meets **Tuesdays**; each appeal is decided within **ten
business days** of receipt. Approve when (a) a mitigating circumstance
outside the student's control is documented and has been resolved or
managed, and (b) the plan reaches the standards by a stated term with a
realistic load. Maximum-timeframe appeals need a degree audit showing
completion within the extended timeframe. Deny otherwise, with the reason
and the path to regain eligibility (attend at own expense until meeting).

## Recording

Record the decision, the plan's per-term conditions and the review date on
the aid record; set the status to *probation* for approvals. At each
subsequent evaluation, check the plan's conditions: met → continue
probation or return to *meeting*; not met → *not meeting*, no further appeal
on the same circumstance.
