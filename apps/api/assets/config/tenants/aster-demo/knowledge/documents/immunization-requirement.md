## What is required

| Vaccine or test | Requirement | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measles, mumps, rubella (MMR) | Two doses after the first birthday, at least 28 days apart, or laboratory immunity | Everyone |
| Meningococcal ACWY | One dose on or after the 16th birthday and within the last five years | Every resident of university housing under 22 |
| Tetanus, diphtheria, pertussis (Tdap) | One dose within the last ten years | Everyone |
| Varicella | Two doses, or laboratory immunity, or a clinician-documented history of disease | Everyone |
| Hepatitis B | Three-dose series | **Required** for Nursing students; recommended for everyone else |
| Tuberculosis screening | Risk questionnaire; test within six months of arrival if indicated | International students and Nursing students (see *Tuberculosis screening*) |
| COVID-19 and influenza | Recommended, not required | |

## How to submit

Upload the record (a clinician's immunization record, a school health form,
or an official registry printout — PDF, JPEG or PNG) on the checklist item
*Provide immunization records*, owned by Student Health and due **30 days
after accepting the offer**. Dates must be legible and the name must match
the student record. Records in a language other than English need a
translation.

## Review

Student Health Services reviews within **seven business days** (its
published service level; most records within three). An accepted record
completes the item. A rejected record shows the reason (illegible,
incomplete, wrong document, expired, information mismatch, evidence cannot
be accepted) and the item returns to *ready*; the due date does not change.
A partial record — for example one MMR dose — is rejected as *incomplete*
with a note naming what is missing.

## What the requirement gates

- **Registration**: a student cannot register until the record is
  **accepted** (or an exemption approved). A record that is uploaded but
  still *under review* does **not** clear the gate — allow the review time
  before the registration window.
- **Housing**: the meningococcal dose is checked at move-in for residents
  under 22; a resident without it may move in but must receive the dose at
  Student Health within 14 days.
- **Health hold**: on **1 October 2026** (Spring entrants: 1 March 2027) a
  health hold is placed on every student without an accepted record or
  approved exemption; it blocks registration for the next term until
  cleared. The hold is cleared the same business day an acceptable record is
  accepted.

## Getting vaccinated

Student Health Services administers every required vaccine at the Wellness
Center (plan members free; others at cost) and documents the dose directly
on the record, so a student who arrives with an incomplete record can
complete it on campus.
