## Official transcripts

Official Aster transcripts are ordered on the Documents page. Each costs
**$8**, payable by card at order time, and is sent electronically the same
business day or by post within three business days. Transcripts are not
released while a **billing hold** (past-due balance over $250) or a
**conduct hold** is on the record; the hold and its owning office are shown
at order time.

A transcript ordered during the final-grade window is held until grades post
unless the student chooses "send now".

## Unofficial transcripts

Available at no charge on the Documents page at any time, including during a
hold.

## Enrollment verification

Enrollment verifications (for insurers, lenders, employers and scholarship
sponsors) are generated on the Documents page at no charge and show
enrollment status by term as of the census date. Verifications for the
current term are available from the first day of classes; full-time status
is certified only after the census date (14 September 2026 / 1 February
2027). Loan-deferment forms are completed by the Records Specialist within
five business days.
