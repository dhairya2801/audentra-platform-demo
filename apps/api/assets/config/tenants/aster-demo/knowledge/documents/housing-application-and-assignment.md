## Eligibility to apply

The housing application opens to a student when the **enrollment deposit
has posted** to the student account (or the deposit is waived). A pending or
in-transit payment does not open it. On the enrollment checklist the item
*Confirm housing plans* stays **blocked** until then; once the deposit posts
it becomes *ready* and the Housing page opens. Students must also be
registered at least half-time (6 credits) by the census date to keep an
assignment.

## Dates (Fall 2026)

| Date | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 15 July 2026 | Application opens |
| 22 July 2026 | Housing accommodation requests due to Accessibility Services (30 days before move-in) |
| **1 August 2026** | **Priority deadline** — applications received by now are guaranteed an assignment |
| 10 August 2026 | Priority assignments published in the portal (hall, room, roommate) |
| 2–22 August 2026 | Later applications assigned in order of receipt as space allows, weekly |
| **22 August 2026** | **Final deadline** — applications after this go to the waitlist |
| 22–23 August 2026 | Move-in |

Spring 2027 admits: application opens 16 November 2026; deadline 8 January
2027; move-in 16–17 January 2027.

## The application

The Housing page asks for a **living plan** (on campus, off campus,
commuting, family, undecided), up to three residence preferences, room type,
bathroom and roommate preferences, a known roommate (name and email, who must
also request you), living-learning community interest, and lifestyle
questions used for matching. *Undecided* keeps the checklist item open;
choosing *off campus*, *commuting* or *family* as a first-year student opens
the residency-exemption form.

## How assignments are made

Assignments are made by the Housing Assignments Specialist in this order:
approved accommodations; first-year students by application date; transfer
and continuing students by application date. Preferences are honoured where
possible but never guaranteed; a student not placed in a preferred hall may
request a room change after the freeze (see *Roommates and room changes*).
Assignments are visible on the Housing page as hall and room (for example
"Fernhollow House 327B") and confirmed by portal message.

## The waitlist

Students on the waitlist are offered space in order as rooms open, by portal
message, with **48 hours** to accept; an unanswered offer moves the student
to the bottom of the list. A first-year student on the waitlist is still
bound by the residency requirement and is placed before anyone else; Housing
guarantees every first-year student a space by the first day of classes,
which may be a temporary triple in Alder Hall converted to a permanent
assignment by the census date.

## Cancelling

See *Housing contract and cancellation*: a cancellation after 1 July carries
a fee, and a first-year student cannot cancel without an approved exemption.
