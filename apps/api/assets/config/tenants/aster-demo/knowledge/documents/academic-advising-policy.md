## Assignment

Every student whose enrollment deposit has posted (or is waived) is
assigned a **primary academic adviser** within **five business days**, by
the Academic Advising Center:

- first-year students: an adviser (Academic Adviser, Senior Academic Adviser
  or Faculty Adviser) whose portfolio matches the **program's department**
  (Computing, Engineering, Business, Social Sciences, Humanities, Natural
  Sciences, Health Sciences, Arts);
- transfer students and Spring admits: a Transfer Success Adviser or the
  **Assistant Director, Transfer & Spring Admits**, drawn from a pool that
  matches the department where possible.

The adviser's name, title, institutional email, office in the Advising
Centre and next open appointment appear on the Dashboard and Appointments
pages. A student with no adviser five business days after the deposit
posted should ask Enrollment Services, which raises it with the Advising
Center the same day. Students also have an admissions counselor and, where
relevant, a financial-aid counselor, an international adviser (DSO) and a
housing coordinator; those are not academic advisers.

## The first advising meeting

The first meeting is **required before registration**. New students book it
on the Appointments page in the **advising window, 10–21 August 2026** (Spring
admits: 4–15 January 2027); the meeting is 30 minutes, in person or virtual,
and covers placement, the general-education core, the first-term plan and
how registration works. The adviser records the meeting, which clears the
advising gate the same day. A student who cannot get a slot with their own
adviser in the window may book any adviser in the same department during
the first-two-weeks drop-in hours (Mon/Wed/Fri 13:00–15:00) — the drop-in
meeting counts.

Continuing students meet their adviser each Fall by **9 October** to plan
Spring; an **advising hold** is placed after that date until they do.

## Adviser on leave or departed

- **On leave** (parental, medical, sabbatical): the adviser's caseload is
  covered by the **Assistant Director, First-Year Advising** for first-year
  students and by the Assistant Director, Transfer & Spring Admits for
  transfers, for the length of the leave. The student's portal shows the
  adviser as on leave with the return date and offers the covering
  adviser's calendar. Appointments already booked are moved to the covering
  adviser.
- **Departed**: the Advising Center **reassigns the caseload within ten
  business days** of the departure date (this is the reassignment rule for a
  departed adviser, distinct from the five-day rule for a new student's first
  assignment); meanwhile the assistant directors cover exactly as for leave. A student assigned to a departed adviser may
  book the covering adviser immediately.
- **No open slots**: an adviser whose calendar is full for the next 14 days
  is flagged to the Director of Academic Advising, who opens overflow hours
  or refers students to the department's other advisers.

## Changing adviser

A student may request a different adviser once per academic year by writing
to advising@synthetic.aster.example with a brief reason; a change of major
to another department triggers a reassignment automatically. Requests are
decided within five business days.

## What advisers do and do not do

Advisers plan courses, interpret the degree audit, endorse overloads and
petitions, and coach on academic standing. They cannot clear checklist
items owned by other offices, change grades, or override registration
gates; they will tell a student which office can.
