## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Biology (**Department of Natural Sciences**,
**124 credits**) is laboratory-intensive; the four extra credits are
laboratory sections. Pre-health students add the pre-health advising track.
STEM-designated.

## Major requirements

| Course | Credits | Recommended term | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BIO 101 General Biology | 4 | 1 | — |
| CHEM 101 General Chemistry I | 4 | 1 | — |
| CHEM 102 General Chemistry II | 4 | 2 | CHEM 101 |
| BIO 210 Genetics | 4 | 2 | BIO 101 |
| BIO 220 Anatomy and Physiology I | 4 | 3 | BIO 101 |
| CHEM 210 Organic Chemistry I | 4 | 3 | CHEM 102 |
| BIO 310 Microbiology | 4 | 4 | BIO 210 |
| BIO 320 Cell and Molecular Biology, BIO 330 Ecology | 8 | 5 | BIO 210 |
| MATH 151 Calculus I; STAT 101 | 7 | 1–2 | placement |
| PHYS 151 University Physics I | 4 | 4 | MATH 151 |
| Biology electives | 8 | 6–7 | BIO 310 |
| BIO 490 Senior Research or Seminar | 3 | 8 | senior standing |

Grade of C or better in every BIO and CHEM course.

## First-year plan

Fall: BIO 101, CHEM 101, MATH 151, WRIT 101, AST 100 (16 credits). Spring:
BIO 210, CHEM 102, STAT 101, one humanities course (14 credits).

## Entry

Open admission; internal transfer requires BIO 101 with C or better.
