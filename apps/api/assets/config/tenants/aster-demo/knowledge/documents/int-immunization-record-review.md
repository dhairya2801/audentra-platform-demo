## Review checklist

For each upload confirm: name and date of birth match the student; MMR two
doses after the first birthday, 28 days apart (or titre); meningococcal ACWY
on/after the 16th birthday within five years for residents under 22 (check
the housing plan); Tdap within ten years; varicella two doses, titre or
clinician-documented history; hepatitis B series for Nursing; TB
questionnaire for international and Nursing students, with a test result
where indicated. Record each accepted component on the immunization
evaluation so a later upload only needs the missing item.

## Decisions

Accept when every required component is met. Reject partial records as
`incomplete` with a note naming the missing dose(s) — never reject a partial
record as `wrong_document`. Use `illegible` for unreadable dates,
`information_mismatch` for a name that differs, `unsupported_evidence` for
a parent's handwritten list. Service level seven business days; target
three during August.

## Exemptions

Route medical and religious exemption requests to the Health Compliance
Manager, who decides within seven business days and records *waived* on the
requirement. Note the exemption on the outbreak-exclusion list.

## Health-hold sweep

On **1 October** (Spring entrants 1 March) run the sweep: every enrolled
student with no accepted record and no approved exemption receives the
health hold, with a portal message naming what is missing. Clear holds the
same business day a record is accepted; the hold-release log is checked by
the Registrar weekly.
