## Advising Centre (rooms 200–224)

Academic Advising Center: every academic adviser's office. Mon–Fri
8:30–16:30; drop-in Mon/Wed/Fri 13:00–15:00 in the first two weeks of term.
advising@synthetic.aster.example.

## Cedarcroft Commons

Ground floor of Cedarcroft House: Housing & Residence Life (Mon–Fri
9:00–17:00; housing@synthetic.aster.example) and the campus mail room for
all halls.

## Global Center

Second floor: International Student Services (Mon–Fri 9:00–11:30 in person;
13:00–15:00 in person or virtual; iss@synthetic.aster.example). First
floor: Study Away office.

## Wellness Center

First floor: Student Health Services (Mon–Fri 8:30–16:30;
studenthealth@synthetic.aster.example) and Accessibility Services (suite
110, Mon–Fri 9:00–16:00; accessibility@synthetic.aster.example). Second
floor: Counseling Services (Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00; urgent 13:00–15:00;
counseling@synthetic.aster.example).

## Student Union

230: New Student Programs (orientation@synthetic.aster.example). 300:
Office of Student Life (studentlife@synthetic.aster.example). 310: Office
of Student Conduct (conduct@synthetic.aster.example). Ground floor: Union
Dining, the bookstore, the ID card office (Campus Safety desk).

## Innovation Hall

120: Career Center (careers@synthetic.aster.example). Computing and
Engineering laboratories and the Computing department office; the
Innovation Hall café.

## Larkin Library

Ground floor: IT Service Desk (helpdesk@synthetic.aster.example) and the
Testing Center. Second floor: Learning Commons — tutoring, the Writing
Center, supplemental instruction (tutoring@synthetic.aster.example). Open
Mon–Thu 7:30–24:00, Fri 7:30–20:00, Sat 10:00–20:00, Sun 10:00–24:00.

## Gatehouse, University Drive

Campus Safety (24 hours; safety@synthetic.aster.example) and Parking &
Transit (permits, the campus shuttle).

## Other places

University Green (events; the Welcome Week Block Party); the Recreation
Center; Birchwood Dining Hall (ground floor of Birchwood Commons); the six
residence halls (see *Residence halls guide*). Aster has one campus, the
Main Campus; there are no branch campuses.

## Telephone

Aster offices are contacted by portal help request, email and in person;
telephone numbers are not published in the portal directory.
