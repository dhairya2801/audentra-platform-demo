## On-campus employment

F-1 students may work **on campus** without separate authorisation once
their SEVIS record is active after check-in: up to **20 hours a week** while
classes are in session and up to 35 hours during official breaks, in any
university department or on-campus contractor (dining, the bookstore).
Postings are on the Career Center's board; the *International Campus
Employment award*, where offered, reserves an allocation but is not a job.
Before the first shift the student needs a Social Security number: the DSO
issues the employment letter within two business days of a job offer, and
the Social Security office appointment follows. Federal Work-Study is not
available to international students.

## Curricular Practical Training (CPT)

Off-campus work that is an integral part of the curriculum (a required
internship, a for-credit practicum) needs **CPT** authorised by a DSO
before it begins: available after one full academic year of full-time
enrollment (Fall 2026 entrants: from Summer 2027), tied to a specific
employer and dates, part-time (20 hours or less) during term. Twelve months
or more of full-time CPT cancels OPT eligibility. Apply on the International
page with the employer's letter and the department's CPT form; decision
within five business days.

## Optional Practical Training (OPT)

Up to 12 months of work in the field of study, before or after completion,
authorised by federal immigration services on the DSO's recommendation;
applications may be filed up to 90 days before the program end date. STEM
programs (BS-CS, BS-DS, BS-ME, BS-EE, BS-CE, BS-BIO, BS-CHEM) may qualify
for a 24-month extension. Information sessions run each October and
February.

## J-1 students

J-1 students obtain written authorisation from the Responsible Officer
before any employment, on or off campus, under the academic-training rules.
