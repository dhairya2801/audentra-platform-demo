## Registration

The checklist item *Register for orientation* (owned by New Student
Programs, due 35 days after accepting the offer) opens once the **enrollment
deposit has posted and the identity document is accepted**; until then it is
*blocked*. Choose a session on the item; confirmation is immediate and can
be changed while seats remain. Registration **closes on 12 August 2026** —
one week before the first session. After that, write to
orientation@synthetic.aster.example: late registrants are placed in the
Remote session if seats allow and otherwise complete the online make-up
module within the first two weeks of term.

## Attendance

Orientation attendance is **required** for all new undergraduate students
(first-year and transfer). It covers registration systems, academic
expectations, campus safety, the honour code and the required Title IX and
alcohol-education modules; the advising meeting is separate and is booked
in the advising window (10–21 August). A student who registers but does not
attend receives a follow-up from New Student Programs within three business
days and is enrolled in the make-up module; the module must be completed by
the add/drop deadline or an advising hold is placed for the next term.

## The fee

The **$175 orientation fee** is charged once to every new undergraduate on
the Fall (or Spring) bill, whichever session is attended, and is not
refundable after the first day of classes. It covers materials, meals during
the session and Welcome Week programming.

## Move-in and orientation

Resident students **move in on 22–23 August, before orientation**, and do
not need to wait for their session; commuting students attend from home.
Families are welcome at the morning of Sessions A–C.

## Welcome Week

28 August – 4 September 2026, opening with the Block Party on University
Green (Friday 28 August, 18:00). Events are on the Campus Life page; none is
required.
