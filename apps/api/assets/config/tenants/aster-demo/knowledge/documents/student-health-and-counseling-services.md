## Student Health Services (Wellness Center, first floor)

Mon–Fri 8:30–16:30. Primary care visits by appointment through the portal
or the nurse line (portal message, same-day reply during hours); walk-in
for urgent illness. Free to members of the university health plan; students
with other insurance are billed at the clinic's published rates. Services:
illness and injury care, immunizations, TB screening, prescriptions,
referrals, travel health, sexual health, and the immunization-compliance
office that reviews checklist records.

## Counseling Services (Wellness Center, second floor)

Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00. Free and confidential for all enrolled students: up to
twelve individual sessions a year, groups and workshops, and referrals to
community providers. **Urgent same-day consultation** is held 13:00–15:00
every weekday, no appointment needed. Visits are never disclosed to
academic, enrollment or immigration offices; a student who wants an office
told something (for example to support a medical withdrawal) signs a
release for that purpose only.

## After hours

Campus Safety (Gatehouse, 24 hours) connects students to the on-call
counsellor and to emergency services. In an emergency, call the local
emergency number first.

## Absence from class for illness

Student Health does not issue class-excuse notes for short illnesses;
students inform instructors directly. For an absence longer than a week, or
a hospitalisation, Student Health or Counseling will, with the student's
release, notify the adviser, who coordinates with instructors.
