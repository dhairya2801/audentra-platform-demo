## Overview

The Bachelor of Arts in Economics (**Department of Social Sciences**, **120
credits**) combines economic theory with quantitative methods; a
quantitative track adds MATH 152, MATH 220 and STAT 310 for students
heading to graduate study.

## Major requirements

| Course | Credits | Recommended term | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ECON 101 Microeconomics | 3 | 1 | — |
| ECON 102 Macroeconomics | 3 | 2 | — |
| MATH 151 Calculus I | 4 | 1 | placement |
| STAT 101 Introduction to Statistics | 3 | 2 | — |
| ECON 320 Intermediate Microeconomics | 3 | 3 | ECON 101, MATH 151 |
| ECON 321 Intermediate Macroeconomics | 3 | 4 | ECON 102, MATH 151 |
| ECON 310 Econometrics | 3 | 4 | ECON 101, ECON 102, STAT 101 |
| Economics electives at the 300 level or above | 18 | 5–8 | ECON 320 or ECON 321 |
| ECON 490 Senior Seminar | 3 | 8 | ECON 310 |

Grade of C or better in every ECON course.

## First-year plan

Fall: ECON 101, MATH 151, WRIT 101, AST 100, one humanities course (14
credits). Spring: ECON 102, STAT 101, two distribution courses, one elective
(15 credits).

## Entry

Open admission.
