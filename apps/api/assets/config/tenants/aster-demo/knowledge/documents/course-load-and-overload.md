## Standard load

| Status | Credits per Fall or Spring term |
| --- | --- |
| Full-time | 12 or more |
| Three-quarter time | 9–11 |
| Half-time | 6–8 |
| Less than half-time | 1–5 |

The normal path to graduation in four years is 15–16 credits a term (30 a
year). Tuition is a flat rate for 12–18 credits; credits above 18 are billed
per credit at the program's per-credit rate.

## Limits

- **First-term students** (any new first-year or transfer student in their
  first Fall or Spring at Aster) may register for at most **17 credits**.
- Any student may register for up to **18 credits** without permission.
- **19–21 credits** requires an **overload petition**: cumulative GPA of
  3.0 or higher (or, for a first-term student, a secondary-school record the
  adviser judges equivalent), the adviser's endorsement and the department
  chair's approval. Petitions are filed with the Academic Advising Center by
  the first day of classes and decided within five business days.
- More than 21 credits is not permitted.

## Dropping below full time

Dropping below 12 credits after the census date has consequences that differ
by student, and the student should check each before dropping:

- **Financial aid**: most aid is recalculated to the credits enrolled on the
  census date; dropping later can create an over-award that is billed back.
  Aster Merit and Aster Access scholarships require full-time enrollment.
- **International students**: dropping below 12 credits without a DSO's
  prior authorisation violates immigration status (see *F-1 enrollment and
  reduced course load*).
- **Housing**: residents must be enrolled at least half-time (6 credits).
- **Health insurance**: the university plan requires at least half-time
  enrollment.

Students who need to study part-time for a term apply through their adviser;
part-time status is approved routinely for documented medical, family or
work reasons and is recorded so aid and immigration offices can act on it.
