## Steps

1. **Accept the offer and pay the enrollment deposit.** The I-20 process
   starts only when the deposit has posted.
2. **Submit proof of funds** on the checklist item *I-20 financial support
   documentation* (international students see this additional item): bank
   statements no older than six months, a sponsor's affidavit and statement,
   or a scholarship letter, showing funds for the first year's cost of
   attendance — **$47,400** for 2026–2027 — less any Aster award at its
   accepted amount. Documents in another language need a translation.
3. **I-20 issued.** A DSO issues the Form I-20 within **ten business days**
   of both items being complete, sends it electronically and records the
   program start date (31 August 2026 for Fall; 19 January 2027 for Spring).
4. **Pay the SEVIS I-901 fee** (currently $350) at the federal SEVP site
   using the SEVIS ID on the I-20; keep the receipt for the visa interview
   and check-in.
5. **Apply for the visa** at a U.S. embassy or consulate with the I-20, the
   fee receipt, the admission letter, proof of funds and ties to home.
   Interview waits vary; students should book as soon as the I-20 arrives.
6. **Travel.** Entry is permitted up to **30 days before the program start
   date** (from 1 August 2026 for Fall). Bring the I-20 in hand luggage.
7. **Check in** with a DSO during the check-in window.

Students already in the U.S. in F-1 status ask their current school to
**transfer the SEVIS record** to Aster (release date on or before the
program start date); Aster then issues a transfer I-20 within five business
days of the release.

## Changes

A change of program, funding source, start date (deferral) or dependants
requires a new I-20 before travel. Lost I-20s are reissued within five
business days.
