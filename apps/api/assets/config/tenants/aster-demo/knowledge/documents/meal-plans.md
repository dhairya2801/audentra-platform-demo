## The plans (per academic year)

| Plan | Code | Meals | Price | Who may choose it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unlimited Access | MP-ANY | Unlimited swipes, plus $150 dining dollars a term | $2,900 | Anyone |
| Fourteen Meals Weekly | MP-14 | 14 a week, plus $200 dining dollars a term | $2,450 | Anyone |
| Ten Meals Weekly | MP-10 | 10 a week, plus $250 dining dollars a term | $2,050 | Anyone |
| Commuter Block of Sixty | MP-COMMUTER | 60 meals a term, plus $100 dining dollars | $850 | Off-campus, commuting and apartment residents |

Residents of **Alder, Birchwood, Cedarcroft and Dunmore** must hold the
Unlimited, Fourteen or Ten plan. Residents of **Elmridge and Fernhollow**
(apartments) and students living off campus may choose any plan or none.
The cost-of-attendance budget uses the Unlimited plan.

## Choosing and changing

The plan is chosen on the housing application (residents) or the Housing
page (commuters) and billed half each term. Changes are made on the Housing
page until the **add/drop deadline** (11 September 2026 / 29 January 2027);
after that the plan is fixed for the term. Unused weekly meals do not roll
over; dining dollars roll from Fall to Spring and expire on 7 May 2027.

## Where to eat

**Birchwood Dining Hall** (all-you-care-to-eat, daily 7:00–22:00) and
**Union Dining** in the Student Union (Mon–Fri 7:00–21:00, weekends
9:00–20:00) take meal swipes; the Innovation Hall café and the Library café
take dining dollars only. Over Thanksgiving and spring break only Union
Dining is open.

## Dietary needs

Aster Dining's dietitian meets students with allergies, coeliac disease,
religious diets (halal and kosher stations are in Birchwood daily) or medical
diets within five business days of a request to dining@synthetic.aster.example.
A medically required release from the resident meal-plan requirement is
approved by Accessibility Services on the dietitian's recommendation.
