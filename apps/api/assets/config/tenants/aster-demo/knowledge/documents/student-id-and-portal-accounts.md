## Student ID number

Every student has an ID of the form SYN-000000, shown on the Profile page
and the ID card; use it on every form and payment.

## Portal account

Created when the offer is accepted; sign-in is by the institutional email
and password or the university's single sign-on. Reset a password from the
sign-in page; for a locked account contact the IT Service Desk (Larkin
Library, ground floor; helpdesk@synthetic.aster.example). Parents or
guardians with FERPA access have their own delegate sign-in.

## Institutional email

The address of record for every official notice (offers, bills, holds,
conduct notices, immigration reporting). Notices are also mirrored to the
portal's Messages page. Forwarding to a personal address is allowed;
missing a notice because of forwarding is not a defence.

## ID card

The Aster ID card is issued at the Campus Safety desk in the Student Union
(or at International Check-In) once the **identity document on the
checklist is accepted** and a photo is uploaded on the Profile page. It
opens residence halls and the library's 24-hour room, carries the meal plan
and dining dollars, and is the shuttle pass. A lost card is deactivated
immediately on the portal and replaced for **$20**; the first replacement
after a name change is free.
