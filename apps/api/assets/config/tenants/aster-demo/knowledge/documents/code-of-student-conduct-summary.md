## Scope

The Code of Student Conduct applies to every enrolled student, on campus, at
university activities anywhere, and off campus where conduct affects the
university community. Residence-hall rules (guests, quiet hours,
alcohol-free floors, prohibited items) are part of the Code and are enforced
first by Housing & Residence Life.

## Principal rules

Prohibited: academic dishonesty (see *Academic integrity policy*);
harassment, discrimination and sexual misconduct (Title IX matters are
handled by the Title IX Office); physical violence or threats; hazing in any
form, including by consent; possession of alcohol under 21 or on
alcohol-free floors, and illegal drugs anywhere; weapons; theft, damage and
unauthorised entry; misuse of university computing or identity; failure to
comply with a university official; and violating a sanction.

## The process

1. A **report** (from staff, students, Campus Safety or the public) is
   reviewed by the Office of Student Conduct (Student Union 310) within two
   business days; frivolous reports are closed.
2. The student receives a **notice** by portal message and email and meets
   a conduct officer within **ten business days**. The student may bring an
   adviser of choice (not a lawyer acting as advocate).
3. Minor matters are resolved by **agreement** at that meeting. Contested or
   serious matters go to a **hearing** before a conduct board of two staff
   and one student within a further ten business days; the standard is
   more likely than not.
4. **Sanctions**: warning, educational assignment, restitution, housing
   probation or removal from housing, disciplinary probation, suspension
   (one or more terms), expulsion. Records are kept seven years.

## The conduct hold

An open matter, or an unfulfilled sanction, places a **conduct hold** that
blocks official transcripts and the diploma. It does **not** block
registration. The hold clears when the matter is resolved or the sanction
completed; Student Conduct clears it the same business day.

## Appeals

Within **five business days** of the decision, in writing to the Director
of Student Life, on grounds of procedural error, new evidence, or a
sanction disproportionate to the finding. The appeal decision is final
except that suspension and expulsion may be appealed further to the Vice
President for Enrollment Management within five business days.

## Amnesty

A student who seeks emergency help for themselves or another student for
alcohol or drug intoxication is not charged with an alcohol or drug
violation for that incident.
