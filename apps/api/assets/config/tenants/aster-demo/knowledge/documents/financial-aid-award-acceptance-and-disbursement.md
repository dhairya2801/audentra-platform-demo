## The award letter

The Financials → Aid page shows every award for 2026–2027 with its **offered
amount**, **accepted amount** and **status**, whether the package is
*estimated* (verification or a document still open) or *final*, the cost of
attendance it was built against, and each condition still holding the
package open. Awards are for the academic year and split evenly between Fall
and Spring unless the letter says otherwise.

## Accepting awards

Each award is accepted, reduced or declined on that page:

- **Grants and scholarships** (Pell, SEOG, State Access, Aster Merit, Aster
  Access, Aster Global) — accepting simply confirms; they never have to be
  repaid while the student stays enrolled.
- **Loans** — may be accepted in part. A first-time borrower must also
  complete the Master Promissory Note and entrance counselling (see *Loans
  and work-study*).
- **Work-study** — accepting reserves the allocation; earnings are paid as
  wages, not credited to the bill.

Deadline: **14 August 2026** for Fall packages (Spring admits: 18 December
2026). Loans not accepted by then are cancelled and must be requested again;
grants and scholarships stay offered but do not disburse until accepted.

## Conditions that hold aid

Aid does not disburse while any of these is open: verification incomplete;
Master Promissory Note or entrance counselling missing (loans only); fewer
than the required credits enrolled (12 for full-time awards; Pell prorates
down to 1 credit); an unresolved satisfactory-academic-progress status
(*not meeting* without an approved appeal); final official transcript still
outstanding at the add/drop deadline; or a missing signed award letter for
State Access Grant. The Aid page names each open condition.

## Disbursement

Aid credits the student account on **Friday 4 September 2026** (Fall) and
**Friday 22 January 2027** (Spring) — after the first day of classes, never
before — and thereafter weekly on Fridays as conditions clear. Federal
Work-Study is never disbursed to the account. Loans for first-year,
first-time borrowers disburse 30 days after the first day of classes
(30 September 2026), as federal rules require.

## Refunds

If disbursed aid exceeds the charges on the account, Student Accounts refunds
the credit balance to the student's bank account (or by cheque to the
address of record) within **14 days** of the credit appearing — normally by
18 September 2026 for Fall. Parent PLUS loan credits go to the parent unless
the parent authorised otherwise. See *Refunds and credit balances*.

## Census recalculation

On the **census date** (14 September 2026 / 1 February 2027) aid is
recalculated to the credits actually enrolled. Dropping below full time
before census reduces the package; dropping after census does not reduce it
for that term but affects SAP.

## Coverage

Whether aid "covers" the bill is a question about the account: the
Financials page shows charges, accepted aid, payments and the remaining
balance. A remaining balance after aid is due on the tuition due date or
through the payment plan.
