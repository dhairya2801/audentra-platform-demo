## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering (**Department of
Engineering**, **128 credits**) is a structured eight-term sequence. The
extra eight credits over the standard degree reflect the laboratory and
design components; students normally carry 16 credits a term.
STEM-designated.

Grade of C or better in every engineering, mathematics and physics course.

## First-year plan

Fall: ENGR 101, ME 110, MATH 151, PHYS 151, WRIT 101, AST 100 (17 credits —
the first-term maximum). Spring: MATH 152, PHYS 152, CHEM 101, one
humanities course (15 credits).

## Entry

Direct admission for applicants who met the mathematics requirement;
internal transfer requires MATH 151 and PHYS 151 with C or better and a 2.5
cumulative GPA. Engineering students are advised by the Engineering pool of
advisers; a student admitted to Engineering without an adviser five days
after depositing should contact Enrollment Services.
