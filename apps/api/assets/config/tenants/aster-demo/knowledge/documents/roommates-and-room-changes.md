## Roommate matching

Roommates are matched from the lifestyle answers on the housing application
(sleep schedule, study habits, cleanliness, noise, guests, temperature) and
mutual requests. Roommate names are published with the assignment on 10
August; students may message each other through the portal before move-in.

## Roommate agreement

Every room or suite completes a **roommate agreement** on the Housing page
within the first two weeks of the term (by 11 September 2026), covering
guests, quiet time, sharing and cleaning. Resident assistants use it as the
first reference in any disagreement.

## Room changes

- **Freeze:** no room changes from move-in until the **census date, 14
  September 2026** (Spring: 1 February 2027), while no-shows and
  cancellations are resolved.
- **After the freeze:** a resident asks their resident coordinator. The
  coordinator first mediates with the roommates; if the situation is not
  resolved within a week, the resident is offered the next available space,
  first in the same hall. A change that moves a student to a different rate
  adjusts the charges from the move date. One change per year is normally
  granted; a second requires the Director's approval.
- **Emergency moves** (safety, harassment, a documented medical need) are
  made immediately by the coordinator on duty, and the situation is referred
  to Student Conduct or Accessibility Services as appropriate.

## A roommate who does not arrive

If the assigned roommate does not check in by the census date the space is
released, and Housing may assign a new roommate at any time with 48 hours'
notice. A resident may request to buy out the empty bed as a single at the
single rate, subject to space.
