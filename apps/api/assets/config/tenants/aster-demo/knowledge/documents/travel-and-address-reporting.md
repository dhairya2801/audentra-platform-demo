## Travel outside the United States

Before leaving, obtain a **travel signature** from a DSO on page two of the
I-20 (page one of the DS-2019). A signature is valid for **twelve months**
(six months while on OPT). Request it on the International page at least
**five business days** before travel; signatures are also given at drop-in
hours Mon–Fri 13:00–15:00. Re-entry requires a valid passport, an unexpired
visa (a visa may be renewed abroad), the signed I-20 and proof of enrollment
or funding. Students on a pending reinstatement or with a terminated record
should not travel.

## Address

An **address change must be reported within ten days** through the Profile
page, which updates SEVIS automatically. Failing to report is a status
violation.

## Other reporting

Report within ten days: a change of major or program; a change in the source
of funding of more than $5,000; a dependant joining or leaving; a name
change on the passport. Ask for a **program extension** at least 30 days
before the I-20 end date if more time is needed to finish; extensions are
granted only for documented academic or medical reasons, not for a change
of major alone.

## Completing the program

After the program end date an F-1 student has a **60-day grace period** to
leave, transfer, change status or begin OPT; J-1 students have 30 days.
