## Overview

The Bachelor of Business Administration (**Department of Business**, **120
credits**) builds a common business core in the first two years and a
concentration (management, marketing, finance, or business analytics) in
the last two, ending with the strategy capstone.

## Major requirements

| Course | Credits | Recommended term | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BUS 101 Foundations of Business | 3 | 1 | — |
| ACCT 101 Financial Accounting | 3 | 2 | — |
| ECON 101 Microeconomics | 3 | 1 | — |
| ECON 102 Macroeconomics | 3 | 2 | — |
| STAT 101 Introduction to Statistics | 3 | 2 | — |
| BUS 210 Organisational Behaviour | 3 | 3 | BUS 101 |
| ACCT 201 Managerial Accounting | 3 | 3 | ACCT 101 |
| BUS 350 Marketing | 3 | 4 | BUS 101 |
| BUS 360 Corporate Finance | 3 | 4 | ACCT 101, STAT 101 |
| BUS 310 Operations Management | 3 | 5 | BUS 210, STAT 101 |
| BUS 320 Business Law and Ethics (also satisfies the core Ethics area) | 3 | 5 | — |
| Concentration (four courses) | 12 | 5–7 | varies |
| BUS 490 Strategy Capstone | 3 | 8 | BUS 310, BUS 360 |

Quantitative reasoning: MATH 120 or MATH 151. Grade of C or better in every
BUS, ACCT and ECON course.

## First-year plan

Fall: BUS 101, ECON 101, MATH 120, WRIT 101, AST 100, one humanities course
(16 credits). Spring: ACCT 101, ECON 102, STAT 101, one social-science
course, one arts course (15 credits).

## Entry

Open admission; Accounting students may switch to the BBA by adviser
request and the reverse requires ACCT 101 with B or better.
