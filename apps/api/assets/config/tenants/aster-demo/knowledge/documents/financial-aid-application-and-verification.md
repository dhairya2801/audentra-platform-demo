## The FAFSA

Federal grants and loans, the State Access Grant and the Aster Access
Scholarship are awarded from the **Free Application for Federal Student
Aid**. Aster's school code is on the Financials page. The priority date for
2026–2027 was **1 March 2026**; the FAFSA is still accepted throughout the
year, but institutional need-based funds are packaged in the order FAFSAs
arrive once the priority date has passed. For 2027–2028 the priority date is
**15 February 2027**.

Students who cannot file a FAFSA — international students, and students
whose citizenship status does not qualify — are considered for the funds in
*International student financial aid* and for the merit scholarship, which
needs no application.

## Verification

Some FAFSAs are selected by the federal processor for **verification**, a
check of the information reported. A selected student sees *Complete
financial-aid verification* on the checklist, owned by Financial Aid, due
**10 days after accepting the offer**. The verification group decides what
is required:

| Group | What is verified | Documents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V1 (standard) | Household size, number in college, income and taxes paid | Verification worksheet; IRS tax return transcript or signed return for the student (and parents, if dependent); W-2s for non-filers |
| V4 (identity) | Identity and statement of educational purpose | Government photo ID presented in person at Financial Aid, or a notarised statement |
| V5 (aggregate) | Everything in V1 and V4 | All of the above |

Documents are uploaded on the checklist item (PDF, JPEG or PNG). Financial
Aid reviews within five business days and rejects with a reason if something
is missing (see *Uploading documents*). Review of a complete file takes **up
to ten business days**; the item then shows *completed* and the award letter
is finalised — awards may go up or down if the verified figures differ.

## What verification does and does not affect

- **Registration**: not affected. A student selected for verification may
  register.
- **Payment plan**: not affected; the student may enrol.
- **Disbursement**: **no federal, state or Aster need-based aid disburses**
  until verification is complete. Aster Merit disburses regardless.
- **The bill**: tuition remains due on 28 August 2026. A student whose
  verification is still open at the due date should enrol in the payment
  plan or ask Student Accounts for a deferment memo, which Student Accounts
  grants automatically when Financial Aid confirms the file is complete and
  only awaiting review.

Verification not completed by the **last day of classes** of the term
cancels the term's need-based aid; a file completed later is considered for
the next term only.

## Special circumstances

A family whose income has changed since the FAFSA year may ask for a
**special-circumstances review** (see *Special circumstances and aid
appeals*).
