## When bills are issued and due

| Term | Bill issued | Due (pay in full or enrol in the plan) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fall 2026 | 15 July 2026 | **Friday 28 August 2026, 23:59** |
| Spring 2027 | 10 December 2026 | **Friday 8 January 2027, 23:59** |

The bill lists tuition (half of the annual rate), mandatory fees, housing and
the meal plan if contracted, the orientation fee for new students, the
health-insurance charge, and any per-credit overload charges, less the
enrollment deposit credit, accepted financial aid and payments already
posted. Accepted aid that has not yet disbursed is shown as **anticipated
aid** and reduces the amount due, provided every condition on the Aid page
is cleared by the due date.

## Ways to pay

Card or bank transfer in the portal (posts within one business day; bank
transfers up to five); international wire through the portal's payment
partner (up to five business days; not late while in transit if initiated by
the due date); third-party sponsor or 529 plan (see *Third-party and 529
payments*).

## The payment plan

The **four-instalment payment plan** spreads a term's balance after aid:

| Fall 2026 instalments | Spring 2027 instalments |
| --- | --- |
| 28 Aug, 28 Sep, 28 Oct, 28 Nov | 8 Jan, 8 Feb, 8 Mar, 8 Apr |

Enrol on the Financials page **by the term's due date**, pay the **$45
enrolment fee** and the first instalment; there is no interest. Enrolling by
the due date prevents a billing hold. A missed instalment has a ten-day grace
period; two missed instalments cancel the plan and the whole balance becomes
past due.

## Past-due balances, the hold and the late fee

- A balance over **$250** that is unpaid after the due date is **past due**
  and places a **billing hold**: no registration, no official transcript, no
  diploma until it is cleared (paid, or a plan enrolled with the first
  instalment paid).
- A balance of $250 or less past due does not place a hold but still accrues
  the late fee.
- A **$75 late fee** posts once the balance is 30 days past due, and the
  account is referred for collection after 120 days.
- A student whose aid is anticipated but held by an open condition (for
  example verification) is not treated as past due while Financial Aid
  confirms the file is only awaiting review — ask Student Accounts for a
  **deferment memo** before the due date.

## The enrollment deposit on the bill

The $500 deposit appears as a credit on the Fall bill. It is not charged
twice; a student who sees "deposit due" on the checklist and "deposit
credit" on the bill has paid once and the checklist item will post shortly.

## Questions and disputes

Student Accounts, Larkin Hall 120, replies within three business days. A
charge the student believes is wrong is disputed in writing; the charge is
not treated as past due while a dispute is open.
