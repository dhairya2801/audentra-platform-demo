## Follow-up cadence for an overdue deposit

| Days past the checklist due date | Action | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | Reminder email (template *Deposit reminder 1*) | Admissions counselor (auto-created work item) |
| 14 | Second reminder plus a call attempt logged as an interaction | Admissions counselor |
| 30, and fewer than 14 days before the first day of classes | Counselor confirms intent; no reply within 5 business days → offer marked *declined – no deposit* by the Associate Director | Associate Director of Admissions |

A deposit that is *pending* (payment submitted, not posted) is never
treated as overdue; check Student Accounts' payment record before any
contact.

## Extensions

A counselor may grant **one extension of up to 30 days** on a written
request citing financial hardship, a pending external scholarship or visa
decision, or a family emergency. Record it on the requirement (new due date
and note) and on the student's timeline; copy Student Accounts so the
billing hold sweep respects it. A second extension requires the Director
of Admissions. Extensions never move the deposit **refund** deadline.

## Waivers

Waive — never defer — for: a FAFSA Student Aid Index of zero (verify in the
aid record), students in foster care (documentation from the agency), and
students with an approved application fee waiver. Record the requirement
as *waived* with the reason code; the student is then treated as deposited
for housing, orientation, adviser assignment and registration. Waivers are
not granted after a payment has posted; refund such a payment through
Student Accounts instead.

## What Edward may and may not do

Staff Edward can draft the reminder emails and summarise the deposit
history; it cannot extend, waive or mark a requirement. Those are portal
actions by the counselor with the reason recorded.
