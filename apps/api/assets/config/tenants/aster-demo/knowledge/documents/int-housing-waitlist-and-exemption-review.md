## Assignment rounds

- **Priority round**: applications received by 1 August are assigned by 10
  August in this order — approved accommodations (from Accessibility
  Services' list), first-year students by application date, transfer and
  continuing students by application date. Honour preferences where space
  allows; record the reason when a first preference is not honoured.
- **Weekly rounds** 2–22 August for later applications, in order of receipt.
- After 22 August every new application goes to the **waitlist**.

## Waitlist

Offer released space to the top of the list by portal message with a **48
hour** expiry; an unanswered offer moves the student to the bottom.
First-year students are always placed ahead of others and must have a bed
by the first day of classes — convert Alder doubles to temporary triples
when needed and record the triple rate. Re-run consolidation after the
census date; students still in triples receive the triple rate for the
term.

## Exemption requests

Decide within **five business days** against the four grounds only: family
within 30 miles (verify the address of record and the family member's
written confirmation), 21 or older on the first day of classes, an
Accessibility Services accommodation that housing cannot meet, or married
/ dependent child / veteran. Financial hardship is not a ground; refer the
student to Financial Aid's special-circumstances review and note it.
Record the decision on the housing requirement (*waived* for approved
exemptions) and notify the student. Late requests: accept only for a
documented change after the deadline.

## Cancellations and releases

Apply the contract fee schedule mechanically; releases during the year
require one of the listed grounds and the Director's approval. Prorate to
the check-out date and notify Student Accounts the same day.
