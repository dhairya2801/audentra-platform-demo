## Overview

The Bachelor of Arts in English Literature (**Department of Humanities**,
**120 credits**) centres on close reading and argument, with a senior thesis
supervised by a faculty member.

## Major requirements

| Course | Credits | Recommended term | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WRIT 101 Academic Writing Seminar (also the core) | 3 | 1 | — |
| ENGL 120 World Literature | 3 | 1 | — |
| ENGL 220 Introduction to Literary Study | 3 | 2 | WRIT 101 |
| ENGL 210 Rhetoric and Argument | 3 | 3 | WRIT 101 |
| Period surveys (three of ENGL 300–309) | 9 | 3–5 | ENGL 220 |
| ENGL 340 Shakespeare | 3 | 5 | ENGL 220 |
| ENGL 350 Modern Fiction | 3 | 6 | ENGL 220 |
| Literature electives | 12 | 5–7 | ENGL 220 |
| ENGL 490 Senior Thesis | 3 | 8 | senior standing |

Grade of C or better in every ENGL course.

## First-year plan

Fall: WRIT 101, ENGL 120, AST 100, MATH 120, one social-science course (13
credits). Spring: ENGL 220, HIST 110, one laboratory science, one arts
course (14 credits).

## Entry

Open admission.
