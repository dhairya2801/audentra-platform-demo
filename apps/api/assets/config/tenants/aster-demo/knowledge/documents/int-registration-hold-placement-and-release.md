## Sweeps

| Hold | Sweep | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Billing | Nightly from the term due date (29 August 2026; 9 January 2027) | Past-due balance over $250 and no active payment plan, no deferment memo, no open dispute, no pending payment |
| Academic-records | Add/drop deadline plus one day (12 September 2026; 30 January 2027) | Final official transcript receipt not logged |
| Health | 1 October 2026; 1 March 2027 | No accepted immunization record and no approved exemption |
| Advising (continuing students) | 10 October 2026 | No Fall advising meeting recorded |
| Advising (new students) | Continuous | Placed at deposit posting, cleared when the first meeting is recorded |
| Conduct | Manual | Placed at case opening, cleared at resolution |

## Release

Clear the same business day the clearing action is verified: a payment
posted or a plan enrolled with the first instalment paid (Student
Accounts); the official transcript logged, or the success plan on file
(Registrar); the record accepted or exemption approved (Student Health);
the meeting recorded (adviser); the case closed (Student Conduct). The
portal reflects releases within minutes; do not tell a student a release
will take days.

## Rules

- Never clear another office's hold; explain it and route.
- Never treat a **pending payment** as past due, and never ask a student to
  pay again while one is pending.
- A hold placed in error is removed by the owning office's director with a
  log note.
- The Registrar reconciles the hold log against the sweep rules every
  Monday and reports discrepancies to the office concerned.
