## Service levels (business days)

| Office (component) | First reply to a student request | Close an Action Center item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enrollment Services (Enrollment Support) | 1 | 1 |
| International Student Services | 2 | 2 |
| Admissions | 3 | 3 |
| Student Accounts | 3 | 3 |
| New Student Programs | 3 | 3 |
| Academic Advising | 5 | 5 |
| Financial Aid | 5 | 5 (verification 10 after complete) |
| Registrar | 5 | 5 (transfer credit 15) |
| Housing & Residence Life | 5 | 5 |
| Student Life | 5 | 5 |
| Student Health | 7 | 7 |

Urgent items (a deadline within 72 hours, an immigration status risk, a
safety concern) are worked the same business day whatever the office.

## Escalation

1. **Stale**: an item *in progress* and untouched for **10 days** is flagged
   by the engagement scan and escalates to the owning office's director,
   who reassigns or closes it.
2. **Owner unavailable**: items owned by a staff member on leave or departed
   escalate to the director on the day the absence is recorded; the
   director reassigns within two business days (advising caseloads: see the
   coverage procedure).
3. **Unassigned**: items unassigned for more than the office's service level
   escalate to the director.
4. **Cross-office conflict** (two offices each waiting on the other, a
   student bounced twice): Enrollment Services raises it to both directors;
   unresolved after five business days, to the Vice President for
   Enrollment Management, who decides within two business days.
5. **Student escalation**: a student replying "escalate" to a help request
   past the service level goes to the office's director the same day.

## Recording

Every escalation is a work-log entry on the item with the reason; the
Morning Brew's people-and-capacity section is built from these logs, so an
escalation not logged is invisible to leadership.
