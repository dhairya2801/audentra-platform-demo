## When to ask

- A parent or the student lost a job, or income fell substantially, since
  the tax year on the FAFSA.
- Unusual costs: unreimbursed medical expenses, a dependant's care, a
  computer required for the program, travel for an international student.
- The student cannot obtain parent information (a **dependency override**
  request: estrangement, abuse, incarceration).

## How

Submit the special-circumstances form on the Financials page with
documentation (termination letter, recent pay stubs, medical bills,
statements from a counsellor or clergy for dependency requests). A
financial-aid counselor reviews within **15 business days** and may recompute
the Student Aid Index or the cost of attendance and re-package the award.
Verification must be complete before a review is decided. Decisions are
emailed and recorded on the aid record; a student may ask the Director of
Financial Aid to reconsider once, within ten business days.

## What a review cannot do

It cannot increase Aster Merit, create eligibility for federal funds for
international students, or override satisfactory-academic-progress rules —
those have their own appeal (*SAP policy*).
