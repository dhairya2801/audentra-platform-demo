## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science is offered by the **Department
of Computing** and requires **120 credits**: the 36-credit general education
core, 54 credits in the major, 12 credits of mathematics and statistics, and
18 credits of free electives. STEM-designated for Optional Practical
Training purposes.

## Major requirements

| Course | Credits | Recommended term | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CS 101 Programming Fundamentals | 4 | 1 | — |
| CS 201 Data Structures | 4 | 2 | CS 101 (C) |
| CS 230 Computer Systems | 4 | 3 | CS 201 (C) |
| CS 310 Software Engineering | 3 | 4 | CS 201 |
| CS 320 Algorithms | 3 | 4 | CS 201, MATH 220 |
| CS 350 Databases | 3 | 5 | CS 201 |
| CS 490 Computing Capstone | 3 | 8 | CS 310, senior standing |
| Six CS electives at the 300 level or above | 18 | 5–8 | varies |
| Ethics designation: PHIL 220 Ethics (also satisfies the core Ethics area) | 3 | 3 | — |

Mathematics and statistics: MATH 151 Calculus I (term 1), MATH 152 Calculus
II (term 2), MATH 220 Linear Algebra (term 3), STAT 101 Introduction to
Statistics (term 2 or 3). Every major course requires a grade of **C** or
better; a course may be repeated once.

## First-year plan

Fall: CS 101, MATH 151, WRIT 101, AST 100, one humanities course (15
credits). Spring: CS 201, MATH 152, STAT 101, one social-science course, one
distribution course (16 credits). Students who place below MATH 151 take
MATH 120 first and start the CS sequence on time — MATH 151 is not a
prerequisite for CS 101 or CS 201.

## Entry and change of major

Open admission; no internal entry requirement. Students in Data Science may
switch by adviser request.

## Advising and contacts

Advisers for Computing majors sit in the Advising Centre; the department
office is in Innovation Hall. The Computing capstone is a two-term team
project with an external partner, arranged in the spring of the third year.
