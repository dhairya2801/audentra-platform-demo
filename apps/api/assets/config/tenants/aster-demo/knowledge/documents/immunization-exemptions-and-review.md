## Medical exemption

Submit, on the immunization checklist item, a letter from a licensed
physician, nurse practitioner or physician assistant stating the vaccine,
the medical contraindication and whether it is permanent or temporary (with
an end date). Temporary exemptions expire on that date and the record must
then be completed.

## Religious exemption

Submit the exemption form (Documents page → forms) with the student's signed
statement that immunization conflicts with sincerely held religious beliefs.
No clergy letter is required. The form covers all required vaccines unless
the student lists specific ones.

## Decision

The Health Compliance Manager decides within **seven business days** and
records the outcome on the checklist item as *waived* (approved) or returns
it to *ready* with a note. An approved exemption clears the registration
gate and prevents the health hold exactly as an accepted record does.

## Conditions of an exemption

Exempt students acknowledge that, during an outbreak of a disease for which
they are unvaccinated, Student Health may exclude them from classes,
housing and campus activities for the incubation period, without academic
penalty (instructors provide remote access or incompletes). Nursing students
with an exemption may be unable to complete clinical placements that
require the vaccine; the School of Nursing advises before enrolment.
