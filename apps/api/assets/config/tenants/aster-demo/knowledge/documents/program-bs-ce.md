## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering (**Department of Engineering**,
**128 credits**) shares the Engineering first year and adds surveying,
structures, geotechnics, transport and environmental systems, with a
site-based senior design project. STEM-designated.

## First-year plan

Fall: ENGR 101, ME 110, MATH 151, PHYS 151, WRIT 101, AST 100 (17 credits).
Spring: CE 210, MATH 152, PHYS 152, CHEM 101 (15 credits).
