## At a glance

| Residence | Style | Beds | Who lives here | Double / single rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Alder Hall** | Traditional (corridor, shared bathrooms) | 320 | First-year students; First-Year Exploration community | $7,250 / $8,600 (triple $6,400) |
| **Birchwood Commons** | Suite (four singles or two doubles around a shared bathroom and lounge) | 260 | First-year and second-year students; Birchwood Dining Hall on the ground floor | $8,100 / $9,400 |
| **Cedarcroft House** | Traditional | 180 | First-year students; Housing & Residence Life office (Cedarcroft Commons) on the ground floor | $7,250 / $8,600 |
| **Dunmore Hall** | Suite | 240 | Second-year and transfer students; Wellness and Recovery community | $8,100 / $9,400 |
| **Elmridge Commons** | Apartment (four bedrooms, kitchen, living room) | 200 | Third- and fourth-year and transfer students | $8,900 / $10,200 |
| **Fernhollow House** | Apartment (two and four bedrooms with kitchen) | 150 | All years; Design Studio and Global Village living-learning communities | $8,900 / $10,200 |

Total on-campus capacity is 1,350 beds. Every hall has a resident
coordinator from Housing & Residence Life, resident assistants on each floor,
laundry, wireless internet, a study lounge and card-access entry. All halls
are alcohol-free on floors housing students under 21 and smoke-free
throughout.

## Room types

Doubles are standard. Singles are limited (about 15% of beds) and go first
to approved accommodations, then by application date. Alder's temporary
triples are used when demand exceeds space and convert to doubles or are
re-assigned by the census date; a student in a triple for the whole term
receives the triple rate.

## Living-learning communities

Living-learning communities place students with a shared interest on one
floor with a linked one-credit seminar and a faculty partner. Applying is a
tick-box on the housing application:

- **First-Year Exploration** (Alder Hall) — for students who have not settled
  on a major; linked to the *Aster Seminar*.
- **Design Studio** (Fernhollow House) — BFA Design and interested students;
  24-hour studio access in the building.
- **Global Village** (Fernhollow House) — international and
  internationally-minded domestic students; linked to the International
  Check-In orientation.
- **Wellness and Recovery** (Dunmore Hall) — substance-free by pledge,
  including students in recovery.

## Meal plans by hall

Residents of Alder, Birchwood, Cedarcroft and Dunmore must hold the Unlimited,
Fourteen or Ten meal plan. Residents of Elmridge and Fernhollow may choose
any plan, including the Commuter Block, or none.

## What is in a room

Extra-long twin bed, desk and chair, wardrobe or closet, and window covering
per resident; suites and apartments add a lounge and, in apartments, a
kitchen with refrigerator and cooktop. Personal refrigerators up to 4 cubic
feet and microwaves up to 900 W are allowed in every room; candles, hot
plates and halogen lamps are not.
