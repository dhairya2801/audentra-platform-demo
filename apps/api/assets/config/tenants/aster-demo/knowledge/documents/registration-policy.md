## Windows

| Term | Registration opens | Registration closes (add/drop deadline) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fall 2026 (new students) | Mon 17 August 2026, 09:00 | Fri 11 September 2026, 23:59 |
| Spring 2027 (continuing students) | Mon 2 November 2026, 09:00 | Fri 29 January 2027, 23:59 |
| Spring 2027 (new Spring admits) | Mon 4 January 2027, 09:00 | Fri 29 January 2027, 23:59 |
| Fall 2027 (continuing students) | Mon 5 April 2027, 09:00 | Fri 10 September 2027, 23:59 |

Within a window each student has a **time ticket**: a start time after
which the student can add courses. New students receive tickets in the order
their advising meetings were completed; continuing students by earned
credits. Tickets are visible on the Classrooms page a week before the window.

## What gates registration

A student cannot add courses while any of these is open:

1. **Enrollment deposit not posted** (or not waived).
2. **First advising meeting not completed** — the advising gate; the meeting
   is booked from the Appointments page during the advising window (10–21
   August 2026 for Fall admits).
3. **No accepted immunization record** and no approved exemption.
4. **A past-due student-account balance over $250** (billing hold).
5. **An academic-records hold** (final official transcript still outstanding
   after the previous add/drop deadline, or an academic-standing review).

Financial-aid verification being incomplete does **not** prevent
registration; it delays disbursement. An open conduct matter does not prevent
registration; it blocks transcripts and the diploma.

The portal's Registration page lists each open gate and what clears it. The
list comes from the student's own record, so it can be trusted over general
statements.

## Course load

Full-time is **12 credits**; the normal load is 15–16. A student may register
for up to **18 credits** without permission. Overloads of 19–21 credits need
an overload petition (see *Course load and overload*). Students on an F-1 or
J-1 visa must stay at 12 credits or more in Fall and Spring (see *F-1
enrollment and reduced course load*). Financial aid requires 12 credits for
a full-time package; aid is recalculated at the census date.

## Full courses and waitlists

A full course offers a waitlist of up to ten. Seats released before the
add/drop deadline are offered to the first waitlisted student by portal
message with **48 hours** to accept. Waitlists are cleared at the add/drop
deadline. Instructors cannot admit a student above the room capacity.

## After the add/drop deadline

Registration closes. A course can then only be dropped as a **withdrawal**
(a W on the transcript, refund by the schedule in the *Withdrawal and refund
policy*) and a course can only be added by petition to the Registrar with the
instructor's and adviser's approval, normally granted only for a course that
began late.

## Who to contact

Time tickets, holds, petitions: Office of the Registrar, Larkin Hall 150.
Course choice and the advising gate: the student's academic adviser. The
deposit and billing gates: Student Accounts. The immunization gate: Student
Health Services.
