## Learning Commons (Larkin Library, 2nd floor)

Free peer tutoring in mathematics, sciences, computing, economics and
accounting (drop-in Mon–Thu 15:00–21:00, Sun 17:00–21:00); the **Writing
Center** by appointment (45 minutes, any course, any stage); supplemental
instruction sessions attached to high-enrollment first-year courses (CS
101, MATH 151, CHEM 101, BIO 101, ACCT 101). tutoring@synthetic.aster.example.

## IT Service Desk (Larkin Library, ground floor)

Portal sign-in and password resets, the campus wireless network, software
licences, loaner laptops (two weeks). Mon–Fri 8:00–20:00, Sat 10:00–16:00.
helpdesk@synthetic.aster.example.

## Career Center (Innovation Hall 120)

Career coaching, résumé and interview preparation (drop-in Wed 13:00–15:00),
the on-campus job board (Work-Study and international campus employment
postings), internships, the Career Fair (spring) and the Internship Ready
Lab in Welcome Week. careers@synthetic.aster.example.

## Campus Safety (Gatehouse)

24 hours every day. Emergencies, the safe-walk escort (any campus location,
18:00–02:00), lost ID cards, building access, incident reports, the
after-hours counseling line. safety@synthetic.aster.example.

## Parking & Transit (Gatehouse)

Student parking permits $180 a year (first-year residents are not eligible
for a permit; exceptions for employment and medical need). The campus
shuttle runs to the town centre and the rail station every 30 minutes,
6:30–23:00, free with the ID card.

## Veterans and Military Services (Enrollment Services, Larkin Hall Lobby)

Certification of education benefits each term after the add/drop deadline,
priority registration, and the veterans' lounge in the Student Union.
Students using benefits are not charged late fees while a benefit payment
is pending.

## Title IX (Larkin Hall 420)

Reports of sex-based discrimination, harassment or misconduct, with
supportive measures (schedule and housing changes, no-contact directives)
available whether or not a formal complaint is filed. Most staff are
required to report what they learn to the Title IX Office; Counseling and
Student Health are confidential.

## Bookstore (Student Union, ground floor)

Course materials (also listed on the Classrooms page), Aster merchandise;
charges may be placed on the student account until the add/drop deadline.
