## Queue

Selected students appear on the *Complete financial-aid verification*
requirement (due 10 days after acceptance). Verification Specialists work
the queue by due date; items past due sort first and are also flagged to
the student's financial-aid counselor for outreach at 7 and 14 days.

## Document decisions

Apply the document review procedure: one reason code, plain-language note.
Verification-specific checks: the worksheet is signed by the student (and a
parent for dependent students); the tax transcript is the correct year and
for every filer in the household; non-filers provide W-2s and a non-filing
statement; V4/V5 identity is presented in person or by notarised statement
— a photo of an ID is *unsupported evidence*.

## Completing the review

Once every document is accepted the file is *complete*; finish the review
within **ten business days**: compare verified figures with the FAFSA,
submit corrections, and re-package if the Student Aid Index changes. Record
*verification complete* on the aid record; the requirement completes and
the award letter status moves from estimated to final. Notify the student
by portal message with any award change.

## Deferment memo

If a complete file is still under review on the tuition due date (28
August 2026), send Student Accounts the **deferment memo** listing the
student and the anticipated aid, so the billing hold sweep skips the
student. Memos are not issued for incomplete files; those students are
directed to the payment plan.

## Deadlines

A file not complete by the last day of classes cancels the term's need-based
aid; the counselor records the outcome and the student's requirement is
retired with reason *term ended*.
