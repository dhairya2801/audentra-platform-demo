## Aster funds

**Aster Merit Scholarship** — awarded at admission on the academic record; no
application. Amounts range from $1,000 to full tuition and are fixed for four
years (eight Fall/Spring terms). Renewal each year requires a cumulative GPA
of **3.00** and **24 credits** completed in the previous Fall and Spring,
checked when Spring grades post (11 May 2027). A student below the line
receives one probationary year; a second year below ends the award. Merit is
not need-based and is available to international students. It is not paid
for summer terms and requires full-time enrollment.

**Aster Access Scholarship** — need-based, from the FAFSA, for domestic
students whose family contribution is below the cost of attendance. Renewed
annually by filing the FAFSA by the priority date and keeping a **2.00**
cumulative GPA with full-time enrollment. Amounts are set each year and can
change with need.

**Aster Global Scholarship** — the institutional need-based award for
international students; see *International student financial aid*.

## Federal grants (domestic students who file the FAFSA)

**Federal Pell Grant** — for undergraduates with exceptional need; the amount
depends on the Student Aid Index and enrollment intensity, with the federal
maximum set each year. Pell is the only award that pays at less than
full-time enrollment (prorated). Lifetime limit: twelve full-time terms.

**Federal Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant (SEOG)** — for Pell
recipients with the greatest need, awarded in FAFSA-receipt order while
funds last; full-time enrollment required.

## State Access Grant

For **in-state residents** (see *Residency classification for tuition*)
who file the FAFSA by the priority date, enrol full time and keep
satisfactory academic progress. The grant requires a signed award letter
returned in the portal each year; without it the grant does not disburse.
A change to out-of-state classification ends the grant.

## Common conditions

Every fund above requires satisfactory academic progress (*SAP policy*) and
disburses only when the conditions on the Aid page are cleared. A student who
withdraws during a term repays the unearned portion under the *Withdrawal and
refund policy*. External scholarships from outside sponsors are reported on
the Financials page and may reduce need-based aid but never Aster Merit.
