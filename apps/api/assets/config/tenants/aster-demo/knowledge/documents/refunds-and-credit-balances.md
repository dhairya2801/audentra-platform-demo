## When a refund is issued

A **credit balance** arises when aid, payments and credits exceed the
charges for the term. Student Accounts refunds it automatically within **14
days** of the credit appearing — for Fall 2026 aid that disburses on 4
September, most refunds are issued by 18 September 2026.

## How it is paid

By direct deposit to the bank account entered on the Financials → Payments
page (fastest; two business days to arrive), otherwise by cheque to the
mailing address of record (up to ten business days). Parent PLUS loan
credits are refunded to the parent unless the parent authorised the student
on the loan application. A credit created by a card payment is refunded to
that card.

## Refunds that are not automatic

- Tuition after a university withdrawal — by the *Withdrawal and refund
  policy* schedule, after the withdrawal is processed.
- Housing and meal-plan charges after a contract release — prorated per the
  *Housing contract and cancellation* policy.
- The enrollment deposit before enrollment — by the *Enrollment deposit
  policy*, from Admissions.

Not refundable: the orientation fee, the payment-plan fee, the late fee, the
health-insurance charge after the waiver deadline, and any charge more than
one year old.

## A refund that has not arrived

Check that the credit appears on the Financials page (aid must have
disbursed, not merely been accepted), that a bank account is on file, and
that no anticipated charge is holding the balance. Otherwise write to
Student Accounts, which traces a payment within three business days.
