## Grading scale

| Grade | Points | Grade | Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 4.0 | C | 2.0 |
| A- | 3.7 | C- | 1.7 |
| B+ | 3.3 | D+ | 1.3 |
| B | 3.0 | D | 1.0 |
| B- | 2.7 | F | 0.0 |
| C+ | 2.3 | | |

Other notations: **P/NP** (pass/no-pass; P is C- or better), **I**
(incomplete), **W** (withdrawal), **AU** (audit), **IP** (in progress, for
multi-term courses). P, NP, I, W, AU and IP carry no grade points. The GPA is
grade points divided by GPA credits attempted; it is computed to two
decimals and never rounded up across a threshold.

## Pass/no-pass

A student may elect P/NP for **one course per term**, declared on the
Classrooms page by the course-withdrawal deadline (6 November 2026 / 2 April
2027) and irrevocable after it. Courses required for the major, the minor or
the general-education core may not be taken P/NP; P/NP credits count towards
graduation but not the GPA. Nursing students may not use P/NP in NURS courses.

## Incompletes

An **I** is given only when a student who is passing cannot finish because of
circumstances beyond their control in the last third of the term, and only
with an incomplete agreement signed by the instructor listing the missing
work. The work is due **six weeks after the first day of the next Fall or
Spring term** (by 9 October 2026 for a Spring 2026 incomplete; by 2 March
2027 for a Fall 2026 incomplete); an unresolved I becomes an F. An I counts
as attempted-not-completed for satisfactory academic progress until it is
resolved.

## Midterm and final grades

Midterm grades post on **16 October 2026** for Fall and 12 March 2027 for
Spring; they are advisory, do not appear on the transcript, and trigger an
outreach from the adviser for any D or F. Final grades are due from faculty
by 17:00 on **22 December 2026** (Fall) and 11 May 2027 (Spring) and post to
the portal the next business day. Academic standing and satisfactory
academic progress are evaluated once final grades post.

## Grade appeals

A student who believes a final grade was assigned in error or unfairly first
asks the instructor in writing within **ten business days** of the grade
posting. If unresolved, the student appeals to the department chair within a
further ten business days, and finally to the dean of the college whose
decision is final. A grade may be changed only by the instructor or through
this process; the Registrar records the outcome.
