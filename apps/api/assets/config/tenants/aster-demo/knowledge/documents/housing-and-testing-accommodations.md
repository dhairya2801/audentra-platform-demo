## Testing accommodations

Exams with extended time or a reduced-distraction room are taken at the
**Testing Center** (Larkin Library, ground floor) unless the instructor
provides the accommodation in class. Book each assessment on the
Accessibility page **at least seven days** before the exam date (final
examinations: by the last day of classes); the instructor is notified and
delivers the paper. Same-day bookings are honoured only when space allows.

## Housing accommodations

Accessibility Services sends approved housing accommodations to Housing &
Residence Life, which builds them into the assignment; the student does not
need to repeat the request on the housing application. Requests by 22 July
2026 are guaranteed; later ones are met as space allows and may mean a
temporary assignment. Approved single rooms are billed at the double rate
where the single is the accommodation itself.

## Dining

A medically required release from the resident meal-plan requirement, or a
modified plan, is approved by Accessibility Services on the Aster Dining
dietitian's recommendation and applied by Student Accounts within five
business days.

## Reduced course load as an accommodation

Where a disability requires fewer than 12 credits, Accessibility Services
certifies a **reduced course load** of 9 or more credits that counts as
full time for housing, institutional scholarships and enrollment
verification. Federal aid is still governed by actual credits (Pell prorates;
other funds require 12 unless the aid office approves a comparable
adjustment), and F-1 students additionally need a DSO's medical reduced
course load. The student meets Financial Aid and, where relevant, a DSO
before dropping.
