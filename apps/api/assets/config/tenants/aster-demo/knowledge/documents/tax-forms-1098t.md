## Availability

Form 1098-T for calendar year 2026 is posted on the Financials → Payments
page by **31 January 2027** and mailed only on request. It reports payments
received for qualified tuition and fees (Box 1) and scholarships and grants
processed (Box 5) during the calendar year; housing, meal plans and the
health-insurance charge are not qualified expenses.

A Social Security number or taxpayer identification number must be on file;
students asked to supply one do so through the Registrar (form W-9S). F-1 and
J-1 students who are non-resident aliens for tax purposes do not receive a
1098-T unless they request it.

Aster does not give tax advice; questions about eligibility for education
credits go to a tax adviser.
