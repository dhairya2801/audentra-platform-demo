## What international students are not eligible for

Students on an F-1 or J-1 visa cannot file the FAFSA and are **not eligible**
for the Federal Pell Grant, SEOG, Federal Direct Loans, Federal Work-Study or
the State Access Grant. Any of those names on an international student's
record is an error to report to Financial Aid.

## What Aster offers

- **Aster Merit Scholarship** — awarded at admission on academic merit,
  renewable with a 3.00 cumulative GPA and 24 credits a year (see
  *Scholarships and grants*).
- **Aster Global Scholarship** — the need-based institutional award for
  international students, from the International Student Financial
  Statement submitted with the application. Renewed annually with full-time
  enrollment, satisfactory academic progress and an updated statement by
  15 February.
- **International Campus Employment award** — an allocation for on-campus
  work of up to 20 hours a week during term, paid as wages (see
  *International student employment*). It is not credited to the bill.
- **International Student Loan** — a private loan through Aster's partner
  lender, credit-based and usually requiring a creditworthy co-signer who is
  a citizen or permanent resident; certified by Financial Aid and disbursed
  on the aid disbursement dates.

All four appear on the Aid page like any other award and are accepted the
same way; the Global Scholarship and the loan disburse on 4 September 2026 /
22 January 2027 once conditions clear.

## Proof of funds

Before an I-20 is issued International Student Services needs evidence that
the first year's cost of attendance ($47,400 for 2026–2027) is covered by
some combination of personal or family funds, sponsors and Aster awards.
Aster awards count at their accepted amount; the employment award does not
count towards proof of funds.

## Billing

International students pay the same due dates as everyone else (28 August
2026; 8 January 2027) and may use the four-instalment payment plan.
International wire payments through the portal's payment partner take up to
five business days to post; a wire sent before the due date is not treated
as late while it is in transit. The **student health insurance plan is
mandatory** and cannot be waived (see *Student health insurance policy*).
