## What check-in is

Every student in F-1 or J-1 status completes **immigration check-in** with a
Designated School Official (DSO) in International Student Services before
registering for classes. Check-in confirms the student has entered the
United States and is present at Aster, which the DSO reports to SEVIS to
**activate** the student's record. Until the record is active the student
cannot register, work on campus or obtain a Social Security number.

## When

| Term | Check-in window | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fall 2026 | Fri 21 – Sat 22 August 2026, 09:00–17:00 | Global Center, 2nd floor |
| Spring 2027 | Wed 13 – Thu 14 January 2027, 09:00–17:00 | Global Center, 2nd floor |

The window doubles as the international orientation (immigration rules,
employment, the health plan, banking, the TB screening clinic and the
English placement assessment for conditional admits). On the enrollment
checklist and the Appointments page it appears as the *international
check-in* appointment.

## What to bring

Passport (valid at least six months), the visa, the Form I-20 or DS-2019 with
the student's signature, the I-94 arrival record (printed from the CBP
site), the SEVIS fee receipt, and the local address. Students transferring
their SEVIS record from another U.S. school also bring the previous I-20.

## Arriving late

Students may enter the U.S. up to 30 days before the program start date on
the I-20 and must report by that date. A student who cannot arrive by the
start date (31 August 2026) writes to iss@synthetic.aster.example
**before** it: a DSO can defer the record by up to 30 days once (to 30
September 2026) if the student can still complete the term's coursework,
which academic departments confirm; otherwise the I-20 is reissued for
Spring 2027 (see *Deferral*). A student who arrives after the deferred date
without contact loses the I-20 and must re-apply.

Late check-ins after the window are by appointment with a DSO, Mon–Fri
9:00–11:30, within five days of arrival.

## After check-in

The DSO activates the SEVIS record within two business days; the checklist
appointment shows *completed*, the registration gate clears (subject to the
other gates), and the student may apply for on-campus employment.
