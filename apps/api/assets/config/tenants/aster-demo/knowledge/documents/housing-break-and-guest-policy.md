## Breaks

- **Thanksgiving (25–27 November 2026)** and **spring break (15–19 March
  2027):** all halls stay open at no charge; dining is limited to Union
  Dining.
- **Winter break (19 December 2026 – 16 January 2027):** halls close except
  for approved break residents. Apply on the Housing page by **4 December
  2026**; the charge is **$30 a night**. Approval is automatic for
  international students, student employees with a supervisor's
  confirmation, athletes in season and students whose home is more than 500
  miles away; others are approved as space allows. Break residents are
  consolidated into Fernhollow and Elmridge; a temporary move may be
  required.

## Guests

Residents may host an overnight guest for up to **three nights in any
30-day period**, with the roommate's agreement recorded in the room
agreement, and must accompany the guest at all times. Guests under 18 need
Housing's prior approval. A guest who violates the code of conduct is the
host's responsibility.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are 22:00–08:00 Sunday to Thursday and 00:00–09:00 Friday and
Saturday, and 24 hours during final examinations. Courtesy hours apply at
all times.
