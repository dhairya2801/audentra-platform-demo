## Tuition (per academic year; billed half each term)

| Classification | Annual tuition | Per term | Per credit above 18 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| In-state | $18,400 | $9,200 | $615 |
| Out-of-state | $28,900 | $14,450 | $965 |
| International | $31,200 | $15,600 | $1,040 |

Tuition is a flat rate for 12–18 credits a term. Fewer than 12 credits is
billed per credit at the same per-credit rate. Summer courses are billed per
credit.

## Mandatory fees (per year, $1,450)

Student activity $220; technology $380; health and wellness $310; recreation
$180; transit and safety $160; records $200. Fees are charged to every
enrolled student and are not waivable, except that students enrolled only in
remote courses pay the technology and records fees only.

## Housing (per year)

| Room type | Traditional halls (Alder, Cedarcroft) | Suite halls (Birchwood, Dunmore) | Apartments (Elmridge, Fernhollow) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Double | **$7,250** | $8,100 | $8,900 |
| Single | $8,600 | $9,400 | $10,200 |
| Triple (Alder only) | $6,400 | — | — |

The cost-of-attendance budget uses the traditional double ($7,250).

## Meal plans (per year)

Unlimited Access $2,900; Fourteen Meals Weekly $2,450; Ten Meals Weekly
$2,050; Commuter Block of Sixty $850. Residents of traditional and suite
halls must hold one of the first three; see *Meal plans*.

## Cost of attendance 2026–2027 (used for financial aid)

| | In-state | Out-of-state | International |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tuition | $18,400 | $28,900 | $31,200 |
| Fees | $1,450 | $1,450 | $1,450 |
| Housing | $7,250 | $7,250 | $7,250 |
| Meal plan | $2,900 | $2,900 | $2,900 |
| Books and supplies | $1,200 | $1,200 | $1,200 |
| Personal and transport | $2,100 | $2,100 | $3,400 |
| **Total** | **$33,300** | **$43,800** | **$47,400** |

Off-campus and commuting students use the same budget for aid purposes;
their bill simply carries no housing or meal-plan charge.
