## First reply

New help requests (status *new*) are worked oldest first from the inquiry
queue. Within **one business day** the specialist either answers, or
acknowledges and routes. An acknowledgement names the office that will
answer and its service level.

## Answer or route

Answer directly: portal navigation, what a checklist status means, how to
upload, where an office is, appointment booking help, profile changes.

Route by topic (the topic code on the request, corrected if the student
chose the wrong one): offer/deposit refund/deferral → Admissions;
transcript, identity document, registration, holds, FERPA → Registrar;
FAFSA, verification, awards, SAP → Financial Aid; bill, payment, plan,
insurance charge → Student Accounts; housing → Housing & Residence Life;
adviser, courses, major → Academic Advising; immunization → Student Health;
visa, I-20, SEVIS → International Student Services; orientation → New
Student Programs; accommodation → Accessibility Services.

Routing = assign the inquiry to the office's pool and create (or link) the
Action Center item for the student, so the owning office sees it in its
queue. Never route by forwarding email.

## Urgent

Mark *urgent* when the request names a deadline within **72 hours**, an
immigration status risk, a safety concern, or a payment that failed on the
due date. Urgent requests are worked the same business day by the owning
office.

## Waiting on the student and archiving

Ask the student for missing information in the thread and set *waiting on
student*. A thread with no activity for **five days** is archived from
inboxes automatically; the record remains and the student may reopen by
replying. Resolve only when the owning office confirms the outcome.

## Privacy

Before discussing a student's record with anyone but the student, verify a
FERPA release for that person and page (see the FERPA verification
procedure).
