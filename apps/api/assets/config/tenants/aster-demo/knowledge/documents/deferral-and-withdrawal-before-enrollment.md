## Deferral

An admitted student may ask to **defer** the offer to the next term (Fall →
Spring) or the next academic year (Fall → Fall). Requests go in writing to
the Office of Admissions before the first day of classes of the original term
and state the reason and the requested term. Admissions decides within three
business days.

Conditions of an approved deferral:

- The enrollment deposit is carried forward and remains subject to the
  original refund deadline; a deferred student who later withdraws does not
  receive it back.
- Institutional scholarships (Aster Merit, Aster Access) are carried forward
  once; a second deferral requires re-application.
- Federal and state aid must be re-applied for with a new FAFSA for the new
  aid year.
- A housing application is cancelled and must be resubmitted for the new
  term; the cancellation fee is waived for an approved deferral.
- International students must obtain a **new Form I-20** with the new
  program start date before travelling; an I-20 is not simply amended.
- A student who enrols elsewhere for credit during the deferral period is
  treated as a transfer applicant and must re-apply.

## Withdrawing before the term begins

A student who decides not to attend tells Admissions in writing. The only
financial consequences are the *Enrollment deposit policy* refund rule and,
for students who signed a housing contract, the *Housing contract and
cancellation* fee schedule. No tuition is owed because none has been charged
before the first day of classes; any tuition already paid is refunded in full
within 14 days.

Students on an F-1 or J-1 visa who withdraw before arriving must inform
International Student Services so the SEVIS record can be cancelled.

## Spring 2027 admits

Spring admits follow the same rules with the Spring dates: deposit refund
deadline 1 December 2026, first day of classes 19 January 2027.
