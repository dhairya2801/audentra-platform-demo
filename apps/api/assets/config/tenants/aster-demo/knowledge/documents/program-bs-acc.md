## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Accounting (**Department of Business**, **122
credits**) shares the business core with the BBA and adds the accounting
sequence required for the professional examination pathway; students who
intend to sit the CPA examination plan additional credits with the adviser.

Grade of C or better in every ACCT and BUS course; ACCT 210 requires a B- in
ACCT 101.

## First-year plan

Fall: BUS 101, ACCT 101, ECON 101, WRIT 101, AST 100, MATH 120 (16 credits).
Spring: ACCT 201, ECON 102, STAT 101, one humanities course, one arts course
(15 credits).
