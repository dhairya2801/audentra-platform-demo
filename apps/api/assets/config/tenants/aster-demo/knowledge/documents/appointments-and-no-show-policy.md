## Who can be booked

From the Appointments page: the student's academic adviser (advising),
admissions counselor (admissions counseling), financial-aid counselor
(financial aid), international adviser (international check-in and
advising), and Enrollment Services specialists (enrollment support).
Health, counseling and accessibility appointments are booked through those
offices' own pages.

## How booking works

Slots are generated from each staff member's **published availability**
(weekly hours, modality, location) minus existing bookings and time off.
Advising and admissions slots are **30 minutes**; financial-aid slots **45
minutes**. Modality is in person (the person's office), virtual (a link
sent on booking) or the student's choice where the person offers either. A
slot that is already taken, outside published hours, or during the person's
recorded absence cannot be booked; the page shows the **next open slot**
and the number open in the next 14 days.

A person on leave or who has left shows no slots; the page routes the
student to the covering adviser or office (see *Academic advising policy*).

## Cancelling and rescheduling

Cancel or reschedule on the Appointments page **at least 24 hours** before
the start; a reschedule keeps the appointment's history. Less than 24
hours' notice counts as a **late cancellation**. Staff cancellations (illness,
emergency) are notified by portal message and email and the student is
offered the next open slot first.

## No-shows

An appointment the student does not attend within ten minutes of the start
is recorded as a **no-show**. After **two no-shows or late cancellations in
a term**, the student can book that office only by contacting its front desk
(for advising, advising@synthetic.aster.example) for the rest of the term.
No-shows never carry a fee. A no-show on the required first advising
meeting leaves the advising gate open; rebook promptly, since time tickets
follow meeting completion.

## Outcomes

After the meeting the staff member records an outcome note the student can
see on the Appointments page (for example "first-term plan agreed; register
from 17 August").
