## The eight requirements

Every admitted student has the same checklist. Due dates are counted from the
day the offer was accepted; the exact date is on each item.

| Requirement | Owning office | Due | Opens after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Verify your profile | Enrollment Services | 7 days | — |
| Provide identity documentation | Registrar | 14 days | profile verification |
| Submit your official transcript | Registrar | 14 days | profile verification |
| Complete financial-aid verification | Financial Aid | 10 days | — (only students selected for verification) |
| Provide immunization records | Student Health | 30 days | profile verification |
| Confirm housing plans | Housing & Residence Life | 18 days | enrollment deposit posted |
| Pay your enrollment deposit | Student Accounts | 21 days | — |
| Register for orientation | New Student Programs | 35 days | deposit posted and identity document accepted |

## What the statuses mean

- **Ready** — open and waiting on the student.
- **Blocked** — a prerequisite item is not complete yet (for example housing
  plans stay blocked until the deposit posts). Nothing is wrong; complete the
  prerequisite first.
- **In progress** — a payment is pending or a form is partly saved.
- **Under review** — a document has been uploaded and is with the owning
  office. Review times: Registrar 5 business days, Financial Aid 5, Student
  Health 7, Enrollment Services 1.
- **Rejected** — the office could not accept what was submitted; the reason is
  recorded on the document (illegible, incomplete, wrong document, expired,
  information mismatch, evidence cannot be accepted). Resubmit; the due date
  does not move.
- **Completed** / **Waived** / **Not applicable** — done. A waiver is recorded
  by the owning office only; a student saying a requirement does not apply is
  not a waiver.

## What happens if a deadline is missed

Missing a checklist deadline never cancels the offer by itself. Each office
follows up, and the consequences are the gates the item controls:

- **Profile verification** — the identity, transcript and immunization items
  stay blocked, so everything downstream is late.
- **Identity document** — orientation registration stays blocked; the
  Registrar cannot release a student ID card.
- **Official transcript** — admission stays conditional; an academic-records
  hold is placed at the add/drop deadline (11 September 2026) that blocks
  next-term registration and Aster transcripts.
- **Financial-aid verification** — no aid disburses until it is complete; the
  bill is due in full on 28 August 2026 regardless, so an unverified student
  should enrol in the payment plan to avoid a billing hold.
- **Immunization record** — registration is gated on an accepted record; a
  health hold is placed on 1 October 2026 for anyone still outstanding.
- **Housing plans** — no room assignment; after 22 August 2026 new
  applications go to the waitlist.
- **Enrollment deposit** — see the deposit policy; housing, adviser
  assignment, orientation and registration all wait on it.
- **Orientation registration** — attendance is required; late registrants are
  placed in the remote session if seats allow and otherwise complete the
  make-up module in the first two weeks of term.

## Where to get help

The Help page sends a question to Enrollment Services, which replies within
one business day and routes it to the owning office. Each checklist item also
names its office. An academic adviser can explain the checklist but cannot
clear an item — only the owning office can.
