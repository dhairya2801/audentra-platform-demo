## The one-stop desk

Enrollment Services (Larkin Hall Lobby, Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00, walk-in or
virtual) is the front door for every enrolling student. The team answers
portal and checklist questions directly and routes anything else to the
office that owns it, so a student never has to work out which office to ask.

## Help requests in the portal

A question sent from the Help page becomes a **help request** with a topic
(getting started, a document, a bill or payment, something else) and a
status the student can watch:

- **New** — received; Enrollment Services replies within **one business day**.
- **Open** — being worked, possibly by the owning office.
- **Waiting on you** — the office has asked a question; reply in the thread.
- **Resolved** — closed; it can be reopened by replying within five days.

Conversations with no activity for five days are archived from the inbox but
kept on the record. A help request never changes a checklist item by itself;
the owning office does that.

## Who owns what

| If the question is about… | It is routed to |
| --- | --- |
| The offer, the deposit refund, deferral | Admissions |
| A transcript, identity document, registration, holds, FERPA | Registrar |
| FAFSA, verification, awards, loans, SAP | Financial Aid |
| The bill, a payment, the payment plan, insurance charge | Student Accounts |
| Housing application, room, contract, move-in | Housing & Residence Life |
| An adviser, course planning, changing major | Academic Advising |
| Immunizations, health holds, exemptions | Student Health |
| Visa, I-20, SEVIS, check-in, work authorisation | International Student Services |
| Orientation | New Student Programs |
| Accommodations | Accessibility Services |

## Appointments

Students can book an adviser, admissions counselor, financial-aid counselor,
international adviser or enrollment specialist from the Appointments page
against the person's published hours; see *Appointments and no-show policy*.

## Escalation

A request unanswered after the office's service level (one to seven business
days depending on the office) may be escalated by replying "escalate" in the
thread; Enrollment Services raises it with the office's director. A student
who disagrees with a decision follows the appeal route in the relevant policy.
