## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Data Science (**Department of Computing**, **120
credits**) pairs a computing and statistics core with a nine-credit domain
concentration (business analytics, biological data, social data, or
computational science) and a practicum with a partner organisation.
STEM-designated.

## Major requirements

| Course | Credits | Recommended term | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DATA 110 Foundations of Data Science | 3 | 1 | — |
| CS 101 Programming Fundamentals | 4 | 1 | — |
| STAT 101 Introduction to Statistics | 3 | 2 | — |
| CS 201 Data Structures | 4 | 2 | CS 101 (C) |
| DATA 220 Statistical Computing | 3 | 3 | DATA 110, STAT 101 |
| MATH 220 Linear Algebra | 3 | 3 | MATH 152 |
| STAT 310 Statistical Inference | 3 | 4 | STAT 101, MATH 152 |
| CS 350 Databases | 3 | 4 | CS 201 |
| DATA 330 Machine Learning Practicum | 3 | 5 | DATA 220, CS 201 |
| Domain concentration (three courses) | 9 | 5–7 | varies |
| DATA 490 Data Science Practicum | 3 | 8 | DATA 330 |
| PHIL 220 Ethics (data ethics section) | 3 | 4 | — |

Mathematics: MATH 151 and MATH 152 in the first year. Grade of C or better
in every major course.

## First-year plan

Fall: DATA 110, CS 101, MATH 151, WRIT 101, AST 100 (15 credits). Spring:
CS 201, STAT 101, MATH 152, one humanities course (14 credits).

## Entry

Open admission. Students may move between Computer Science and Data Science
by adviser request; the two share the first year.
