## What is protected

A student's education records — grades, enrollment, financial-aid and
account information, documents, advising notes and health-compliance status
— are private. Aster releases them only to the student, to university
officials with a legitimate educational interest, and to a third party the
student has authorised in writing.

## Parent or guardian access

Parents and guardians have no automatic access, whatever the student's age or
who pays the bill. During onboarding the student signs a **FERPA release**
and chooses, for each parent or guardian, exactly which portal pages that
person may see: enrollment and checklist, documents, financials and payments,
appointments, campus life, messages, and the Edward assistant. The person
then receives a delegate sign-in that shows only those pages.

The student can change or revoke a release at any time on the Enrollment →
FERPA page; a change takes effect immediately. A parent who contacts an
office directly is given only the information the release covers, after the
office verifies the release on the record (see the internal *FERPA release
verification* procedure).

## Directory information

Aster may publish directory information — name, program, dates of
attendance, degrees and honours, and participation in officially recognised
activities — unless the student opts out on the Profile page. Opting out also
removes the student from enrollment-verification lookups by employers and
insurers, so it should be considered carefully.

## Inspecting and correcting records

A student may inspect their records by written request to the Registrar,
which is fulfilled within **45 days**. A student who believes a record is
inaccurate may ask for a correction; if the Registrar declines, the student
may request a hearing. Grades are contested through the *Grading policy*
appeal, not through FERPA.

## Health and counseling records

Records of Student Health and Counseling Services are treatment records, kept
apart from the education record; only the compliance status (accepted,
exempt, outstanding) is visible to enrollment offices.
