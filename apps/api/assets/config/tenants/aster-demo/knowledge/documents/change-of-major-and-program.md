## Changing major

1. Meet the current adviser to review how completed and planned courses
   apply to the new program (the degree audit on the Classrooms page shows
   this for any program).
2. Submit the change-of-major form on the Classrooms page. Programs without
   entry requirements (Economics, Psychology, English Literature, Business
   Administration, Accounting, Computer Science, Data Science, Biology,
   Chemistry) are approved by the Advising Center within five business
   days.
3. Programs with entry requirements go to the department for review within
   ten business days:
   - **Nursing (BSN)**: a 3.0 cumulative GPA, BIO 101 and CHEM 101 with B or
     better, seats available in the clinical cohort; entry is in Fall only.
   - **Engineering (BS-ME, BS-EE, BS-CE)**: MATH 151 and PHYS 151 with C or
     better and a 2.5 cumulative GPA.
   - **Design (BFA-DES)**: a portfolio review by the Department of Arts.
4. The change takes effect at the start of the **next term**; the adviser is
   reassigned if the department changes, and the new program's catalog year
   is the year of the change.

Changes are reported automatically to Financial Aid (some awards are
program-specific) and appear on the international student's record for the
DSO to update SEVIS within ten days.

## Minors and double majors

A minor (18–21 credits) or a second major is declared the same way; a
double major must be completed within the maximum timeframe for aid (150%
of the primary program) and needs the adviser's plan showing how.

## Undecided students

Students may enter as *undeclared* within a college and must declare a
major by the end of the fourth term (60 credits). The First-Year
Exploration living-learning community and the Aster Seminar are designed
for them.
