## Standings

Academic standing is the Registrar's measure of a student's academic
record. It is separate from *satisfactory academic progress* (SAP), the
Financial Aid measure that decides aid eligibility, although both use the
2.0 cumulative-GPA line.

| Standing | Definition | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Good standing | Cumulative GPA 2.00 or higher and last term GPA 2.00 or higher | Nothing |
| Academic warning | Cumulative GPA 2.00 or higher but the last term GPA below 2.00 | Required meeting with the adviser before next registration; success-plan recommended |
| Academic probation | Cumulative GPA below 2.00 | Registration limited to 13 credits; **academic-standing review hold** placed until the adviser meeting and a signed success plan are on file |
| Continued probation | Second consecutive term with cumulative GPA below 2.00 but term GPA 2.00 or higher | As probation |
| Academic dismissal | Cumulative and term GPA both below 2.00 in two consecutive terms | Not permitted to register; may appeal |

Standing is evaluated after each Fall and Spring term when final grades
post (22 December 2026; 11 May 2027). New students are in good standing
until their first evaluation.

## Appeal of dismissal

A dismissed student may appeal in writing to the **Academic Standards
Committee** within **ten business days** of the dismissal notice, describing
the circumstances and a concrete plan. The committee meets in the second and
fourth weeks of January, June and August and decides before the next term's
add/drop deadline. A successful appeal reinstates the student on continued
probation with a 13-credit limit and a required weekly check-in with the
adviser for the term.

## Return after dismissal

Without a successful appeal a dismissed student may apply for readmission
after **two full terms** away, with evidence of readiness (for example
coursework elsewhere with a 2.5 GPA). Readmission is through Admissions and
is not guaranteed.

## Scholarships and aid

Probation does not by itself end an institutional scholarship, but the
scholarships' renewal criteria (cumulative 3.0 for Aster Merit, 2.0 for
Aster Access) are checked at the same evaluation, and SAP is evaluated on the
same grades; see the *Satisfactory academic progress policy*.
