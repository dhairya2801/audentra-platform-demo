## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Nursing (**Department of Health Sciences**, **130
credits**) is a pre-licensure program: the first year is pre-nursing
science, clinical courses begin in the third term, and the program ends
with a capstone practicum. Entry is in Fall only and cohorts are limited to
clinical capacity.

## Progression requirements

Beyond the general rules, Nursing students must:

- keep a **3.0 cumulative GPA** and earn **B- or better** in every NURS,
  BIO and CHEM course (one repeat allowed across the program);
- complete the **hepatitis B** series (required, not merely recommended), a
  current influenza vaccine, and **annual tuberculosis screening** before
  each clinical year;
- hold current **CPR certification** (healthcare-provider level) and pass a
  **criminal background check and drug screen** arranged by the department
  before the first clinical placement (term 3);
- not use pass/no-pass for any NURS course.

A student who falls below the progression standard is placed on program
probation for one term; a second term ends enrollment in the BSN with a
transfer to the BS in Biology or another program.

## First-year plan

Fall: BIO 101, CHEM 101, NURS 101, WRIT 101, AST 100 (15 credits). Spring:
BIO 220, PSY 101, STAT 101, one humanities course (13 credits).

## Entry

Direct admission to the BSN at application; internal transfer needs a 3.0
GPA, BIO 101 and CHEM 101 with B or better, and a seat in the Fall cohort.
