## The rule

All **first-year undergraduate students** — students entering Aster
directly from secondary school, whatever their age or credits earned
through examinations — live in university housing for **both semesters of
their first academic year** and hold a resident meal plan. The requirement
follows the student, not the term: a first-year student who enters in Spring
2027 lives on campus for Spring 2027 and Fall 2027.

Transfer students are not subject to the requirement; they may apply for
housing and are assigned as space allows after first-year students.

## Exemptions

An exemption is granted only for one of these:

1. the student lives with a **parent, legal guardian or other immediate
   family member within 30 miles** of campus (the address must be the
   student's address of record and the family member confirms in writing);
2. the student is **21 years of age or older on the first day of classes**;
3. the student has an **approved accommodation** from Accessibility Services
   that cannot be met in university housing;
4. the student is married or has a dependent child living with them, or is a
   veteran with more than one year of active service.

Financial hardship is not by itself an exemption; a student who cannot afford
housing should ask Financial Aid for a special-circumstances review, which
considers housing costs.

## How and when to request

Submit the exemption request on the Housing page — the "living plan" answer
*commuting* or *family* opens the form — with the supporting document, **on
or before the housing application deadline, 22 August 2026** (Spring
entrants: 8 January 2027). Housing & Residence Life decides within five
business days and records the outcome on the housing requirement.

A request filed after the deadline is considered only for a documented change
of circumstances that occurred after it. A first-year student who has not
applied for housing and has no approved exemption by the deadline is assigned
to available space and charged for it.

## Living off campus in the second year

The requirement ends after the first year. Students who wish to stay in
housing sign a new contract in the spring room-selection process; nothing
requires it.
