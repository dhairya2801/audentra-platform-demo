## Sponsors and employers

Upload the sponsor's authorisation letter (stating what it covers and the
billing address) on the Financials page **by the tuition due date**. Student
Accounts invoices the sponsor directly and records the sponsored amount as
anticipated payment, so it does not count as past due. If the sponsor has
not paid within **60 days** of the invoice, the amount returns to the
student's balance with the normal past-due rules from that date.
Embassy-sponsored international students follow the same process and may
also be exempt from the university health plan on the sponsor's letter.

## 529 plans

Request the distribution from the plan administrator, payable to Aster
University with the student ID on the payment, at least ten business days
before the due date. Upload the confirmation on the Financials page so the
amount shows as anticipated. Overpayments are refunded to the student unless
the plan requires a return.

## Employer tuition benefits paid after grades

If an employer reimburses only after grades are posted, the student pays by
the due date (the payment plan is available) and is reimbursed by the
employer; Aster does not defer for post-term reimbursement.
