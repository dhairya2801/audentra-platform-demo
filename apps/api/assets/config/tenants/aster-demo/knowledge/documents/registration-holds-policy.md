## The five holds

| Hold | Placed by | Placed when | Blocks | Cleared when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Billing hold** | Student Accounts | A balance over $250 is past due (after 28 August 2026 for Fall charges; 8 January 2027 for Spring) | Registration, official transcripts, diploma | The past-due amount is paid or a payment plan is enrolled and the first instalment paid |
| **Academic-records / standing review hold** | Registrar | The final official transcript is still outstanding at the add/drop deadline, or the student is on academic probation without a signed success plan | Registration, official transcripts | The official transcript is received; or the adviser meeting and success plan are on file |
| **Health hold** | Student Health | No accepted immunization record or approved exemption by 1 October 2026 (Spring admits: 1 March 2027) | Registration | The record is accepted or the exemption approved |
| **Advising hold** | Academic Advising | New students: until the first advising meeting is completed. Continuing students: the Fall advising meeting is not done by 9 October 2026 | Registration | The advising meeting is recorded by the adviser |
| **Conduct hold** | Student Conduct | An open conduct matter, or an unfulfilled sanction | Official transcripts, diploma (not registration) | The matter is resolved or the sanction completed |

Only the owning office can place or clear its hold; another office can
explain it but not remove it. Holds appear on the Registration page with the
owning office and the clearing action.

## Blockers are not holds

Before the first term, most of what stops a new student is an **enrollment
blocker** — an open checklist item such as the deposit or the immunization
record — rather than a hold. The portal lists these as blockers with their
office and clearing action; they become the health or billing hold above only
on the dates in the table. An enrollment blocker never affects transcripts.

## Timing

A hold cleared before the student's registration time ticket has no effect.
A hold placed during a registration window blocks further adds but does not
remove courses already added. Offices clear holds on the same business day
the clearing action is verified; the portal reflects the change within
minutes.
