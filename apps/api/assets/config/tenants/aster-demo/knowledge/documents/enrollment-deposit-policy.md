## Summary

Every admitted undergraduate pays a **$500 enrollment deposit** to confirm the
offer. The deposit is credited in full against first-semester tuition on the
student account; it is not an extra charge. It appears on the enrollment
checklist as *Pay your enrollment deposit*, owned by the Office of Student
Accounts, and it is due **21 days after the offer is accepted** (the exact
date is on the checklist item).

## What the deposit unlocks

The deposit must have **posted** to the student account — not merely been
submitted — before the following open:

- the housing application and any room assignment (Housing & Residence Life);
- orientation registration (New Student Programs), which also needs the
  identity document accepted;
- assignment of an academic adviser (within five business days of posting);
- course registration.

A card payment posts within one business day; a bank transfer or a payment
sent by a third party can take up to five business days. While a payment is
*pending* the student is not asked to pay again and should not: the checklist
item shows "processing" until it posts.

## Missing the deposit deadline

The deposit deadline on the checklist is an **institutional deadline, not a
forfeiture date**. A student who misses it keeps the offer, but:

- the housing application stays closed, so a late deposit moves the student
  behind everyone who applied for housing on time (after 22 August 2026 that
  means the waitlist for Fall 2026);
- no adviser is assigned and no registration time ticket is issued, which
  delays the first advising meeting and therefore registration;
- Admissions follows up by email at 7 and 14 days past due; a deposit still
  missing 30 days after the deadline and 14 days before the first day of
  classes is treated as a declined offer unless the student has asked
  Admissions in writing for an extension.

Extensions of up to 30 days are granted by an admissions counselor on request
for financial hardship or a pending external decision; see *Deposit extension
and waiver procedure*.

## Refund and forfeiture

The deposit is **refundable** only when a written withdrawal request reaches
the Office of Admissions (admissions@synthetic.aster.example or the portal
help request) on or before:

| Admit term | Full-refund deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Fall 2026 | 1 June 2026 |
| Spring 2027 | 1 December 2026 |

After that date the deposit is **non-refundable** and is forfeited if the
student does not enrol. A student who enrols and later withdraws is covered by
the *Withdrawal and refund policy*, not by this section: the deposit has by
then become tuition credit.

## Waivers

The deposit is waived — never deferred — for students whose FAFSA shows a
Student Aid Index of zero, students in foster care, and students with an
approved Admissions fee-waiver on file. A waiver is recorded on the checklist
item as *waived*; a waived student is treated as deposited for every gate
above. Students who believe they qualify write to Admissions before the
deposit deadline; waivers are not granted retroactively after a payment.

- A payment that has not posted, a card that was declined, a payment plan:
