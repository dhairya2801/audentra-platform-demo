## Clubs

The Campus Life page lists every registered club with its category,
contact, meeting schedule and whether membership is open. Joining is free:
choose *interested* on the club and the club's contact reaches out. Clubs
are registered by the Office of Student Life (Student Union 300) each
September and must have at least five members, a constitution and a staff
or faculty adviser; a new club may be proposed at any time and is
registered within ten business days. Clubs may book rooms and request
funding from the student activity fee through Student Life.

## Events

Campus events are published on the Campus Life page with date, time,
location and, where seats are limited, registration. Registering is
free; a registered student is notified by portal message if the event's
time, place or availability changes or it is cancelled. Featured events for
2026–2027 include the Welcome Week Block Party (28 August), the Involvement
Fair (2 September), the First-Year Research Showcase (2 September), the
Internship Ready Lab (9 September), Family Weekend (16–18 October),
Homecoming (23–24 October), the Study Away Fair (4 November), the Winter
Concert (10 December), the Career Fair (24 February 2027), the Spring
Festival (16 April 2027) and Senior Week (10–14 May 2027).

## Conduct

Clubs and their events are subject to the Code of Student Conduct; Student
Life is the liaison to Student Conduct for organisation matters, and hazing
in any form ends a club's registration.
