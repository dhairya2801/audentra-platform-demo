## The requirement

To keep F-1 or J-1 status a student must be enrolled **full time — at least
12 credits — in every Fall and Spring term** and make normal progress
towards the degree. At most **3 credits** of fully online coursework count
towards the 12 in any term. Summer enrollment is optional unless summer is
the first term.

## Reduced course load (RCL)

A DSO may authorise, **in advance**, enrollment below 12 credits in these
cases only:

| Reason | Limits |
| --- | --- |
| Initial academic difficulty (English language, unfamiliarity with U.S. teaching, improper course placement) | Once per degree level, in the first term only; at least 6 credits |
| Medical condition (letter from a licensed physician, psychologist or clinical psychologist) | Up to 12 months in total; may be zero credits |
| Final term, fewer credits needed to graduate | Once; at least the credits required |

Apply on the International page (portal) with the documentation and the
adviser's confirmation; the DSO decides within **two business days** and
records the authorisation in SEVIS. Dropping a course before the
authorisation exists is a status violation even if the RCL is later
approved.

## Consequences of falling below full time without authorisation

The DSO must report the student as out of status; the student loses
on-campus employment eligibility and must either leave the U.S. or apply for
reinstatement (a lengthy federal process). Financial-aid and housing rules
about credits also apply. Always speak to a DSO before dropping.

## Related rules

Students in WRIT 099 Academic English under a conditional admission are
placed on an initial-difficulty RCL by the DSO automatically. A withdrawal
from the university is reported as an authorised early withdrawal only if a
DSO is consulted first (see *Withdrawal and refund policy*).
