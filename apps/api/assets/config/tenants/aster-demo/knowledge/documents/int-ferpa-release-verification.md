## The check

1. Open the student's Enrollment → FERPA record. Confirm the person is a
   **named delegate** with a **current** release.
2. Confirm the **scope**: each release lists pages (enrollment and
   checklist, documents, financials and payments, appointments, campus
   life, messages, assistant). Discuss only what the scope covers — a
   release for enrollment does not cover the bill.
3. Verify the caller's identity: full name as on the release and the
   student's ID number or date of birth. Email from the delegate's address
   on the release counts as identity.

## No release, or out of scope

Share only **directory information** (name, program, dates of attendance)
unless the student has opted out, in which case confirm nothing. Say that
the student can add or extend the release in the portal in minutes; do not
ask the student to do so by proxy over the phone.

## Emergencies

A health or safety emergency permits disclosure of what is necessary to
protect the student; the Registrar records the disclosure and its reason
within one business day.

## Logging

Record every disclosure to a delegate as an interaction on the student
record (channel, person, topic). Staff Edward answers a staff member's
question about a student only from the staff member's own authorisation; it
does not check FERPA for third parties — the human does.
