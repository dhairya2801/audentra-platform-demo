## Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Chemistry (**Department of Natural Sciences**,
**124 credits**) is laboratory-intensive and ends in a two-term research
capstone. STEM-designated.

## Major requirements

| Course | Credits | Recommended term | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CHEM 101 General Chemistry I | 4 | 1 | — |
| CHEM 102 General Chemistry II | 4 | 2 | CHEM 101 |
| MATH 151, MATH 152 Calculus I–II | 8 | 1–2 | placement |
| PHYS 151, PHYS 152 University Physics I–II | 8 | 2–3 | MATH 151 |
| CHEM 210 Organic Chemistry I | 4 | 3 | CHEM 102 |
| CHEM 211 Organic Chemistry II | 4 | 4 | CHEM 210 |
| CHEM 320 Analytical Chemistry | 4 | 4 | CHEM 102 |
| CHEM 310 Physical Chemistry | 3 | 5 | CHEM 102, MATH 152, PHYS 151 |
| CHEM 330 Inorganic Chemistry, CHEM 340 Biochemistry | 8 | 5–6 | CHEM 211 |
| Chemistry electives | 6 | 6–7 | CHEM 310 |
| CHEM 490–491 Research Capstone I–II | 6 | 7–8 | CHEM 310 |

Grade of C or better in every CHEM, MATH and PHYS course.

## First-year plan

Fall: CHEM 101, MATH 151, WRIT 101, AST 100, one humanities course (15
credits). Spring: CHEM 102, MATH 152, PHYS 151, one social-science course
(15 credits).

## Entry

Open admission.
