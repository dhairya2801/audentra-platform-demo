## Overview

The Bachelor of Fine Arts in Design (**Department of Arts**, **126
credits**) is a studio degree: 66 credits of studio coursework, 24 of art
history and theory, the 36-credit core. Studio courses meet six hours a
week for three credits. The Design Studio living-learning community in
Fernhollow House offers 24-hour studio access.

| DES 310 Interaction Design | 3 | 5 | DES 210 |

| Art history and theory electives | 12 | 4–7 | ART 101 |
| Studio electives | 12 | 6–7 | DES 310 |
| DES 490 Senior Thesis Exhibition | 6 | 8 | DES 330 |

Grade of C or better in every DES course; the sophomore portfolio review at
the end of term 4 is required to continue into 300-level studios.

## First-year plan

Fall: DES 101, DES 120, ART 101, WRIT 101, AST 100 (13 credits). Spring: DES
130, DES 140, MATH 120, one humanities course (12 credits — studio students
carry fewer credits for more contact hours).

## Entry

Admission by portfolio at application; internal transfer by portfolio
review with the Department of Arts, offered each term.
