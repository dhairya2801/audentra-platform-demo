## Accepting the offer

An offer of admission is accepted in the portal (*Review your offer*), which
records the program, starting term and campus, and starts the enrollment
checklist. The **response deadline** printed on the offer letter and shown on
the enrollment page is the last day to accept; an offer not accepted by then
lapses and can be reinstated only by writing to Admissions, subject to space
in the program.

Accepting the offer does not by itself reserve the place — the enrollment
deposit does (see *Enrollment deposit policy*).

## Conditional admission

Every offer is **conditional** on two things:

1. **Successful completion of the credential the offer was based on** (the
   secondary-school diploma or, for transfer students, the coursework in
   progress at the time of application) with no material change in
   performance. Admissions may rescind an offer if the final record differs
   materially from the application — for example a failed graduation
   requirement or a term GPA more than 0.5 below the application GPA. A
   rescission is always preceded by a written request for explanation with
   ten business days to respond.
2. **Receipt of a final official transcript** by the Office of the Registrar.

## What "official" means

A transcript is official when the issuing school sends it **directly** to
Aster — electronically through a transcript service to
registrar@synthetic.aster.example, or in the school's sealed envelope to the
Office of the Registrar, Larkin Hall 150. A transcript the student uploads to
the portal is accepted **for review only**: it lets the Registrar start
transfer-credit evaluation and clears the checklist item to *under review*,
but the requirement is not satisfied until the official copy arrives. The
checklist item *Submit your official transcript* is due 14 days after the
offer is accepted.

For transfer students the same rule applies to each post-secondary
institution attended, including institutions whose credit is not sought.

## Deadlines and consequences

- The transcript should arrive **before the first day of classes**.
- A student whose final official transcript has not been received by the
  **add/drop deadline (11 September 2026 for Fall; 29 January 2027 for
  Spring)** receives an **academic-records hold** placed by the Registrar. The
  hold blocks registration for the following term and the release of Aster
  transcripts; it does not remove the student from current courses.
- A transcript rejected in review (illegible, incomplete, wrong document,
  information mismatch) must be resubmitted; the rejection reason is shown on
  the document and the checklist item returns to *ready*.

## Who to contact

Questions about the offer, the response deadline or rescission: Office of
Admissions. Questions about whether a transcript has arrived, transfer credit,
or a rejected upload: Office of the Registrar (transcript evaluators). The
one-stop Enrollment Services desk can check the status of either on a
student's behalf.
