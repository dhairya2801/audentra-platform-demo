## Accepted files

Each checklist upload accepts **PDF, JPEG or PNG** files up to **10 MB**. A
multi-page transcript may be one PDF or several images. Photographs must show
every edge of the page; screenshots of a portal are accepted for unofficial
transcripts only.

## Review

An upload moves the checklist item to *under review*. The owning office
reviews within its service level (Registrar and Financial Aid 5 business
days, Student Health 7, Enrollment Services 1). The platform extracts the
document's key fields automatically; extracted fields are suggestions that a
reviewer confirms — nothing is accepted without a person's decision.

## Rejection reasons

If a document cannot be accepted the reviewer records exactly one reason,
shown on the document in the portal:

| Reason | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Image or text is unclear | Blurred, cropped or dark scan | Re-scan flat, in good light, with every edge visible |
| Document is incomplete | Pages, signatures or required fields missing | Upload every page; sign where required |
| Wrong document | Not the document this task asks for (e.g. a class schedule instead of a transcript) | Upload the requested document |
| Document is expired | Identity document or insurance card past its validity | Upload a currently valid document |
| Information does not match | Name, date of birth or school differs from the application | Correct the record with the Registrar first, or upload a matching document |
| Evidence cannot be accepted | An unofficial or self-attested source where an official one is required | Obtain the official document |

A rejection returns the item to *ready*; **the original due date does not
move**. Resubmissions are reviewed in the same service level, and a document
rejected twice is escalated to the office's director for a call or virtual
meeting with the student.

## Identity documents

Accepted identity documents: passport, national identity card, driver's
licence or state identity card, or a permanent-resident card. The document
must be valid on the day it is reviewed and the name must match the
application; a name change is corrected through the Registrar before upload.

## Privacy

Uploads are stored encrypted and visible only to the owning office and to the
student (and to a parent or guardian only for the pages the student's FERPA
release covers). Documents are retained for the enrollment record and never
returned; do not upload originals that cannot be replaced.
