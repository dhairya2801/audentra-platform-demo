## Preferred name and pronouns

Change on the Profile page at any time; the change applies immediately to
the portal, class rosters, the ID card (a replacement is free once) and
email display names. It does not change the legal name on the transcript,
diploma, financial-aid or immigration records.

## Legal name

A legal name change requires one government document showing the new name
(passport, court order, marriage certificate, updated driver's licence),
uploaded on the Documents page under "identity". The Registrar reviews it
within five business days and updates every record. International students
must also bring the document to International Student Services so the SEVIS
record matches the passport.

## Contact details

Email and mobile phone are changed on the Profile page and verified by a
code sent to the new address or number. The institutional email address is
the address of record for every official notice.

## Mailing address

Update within **ten days** of moving. For F-1 and J-1 students this is an
immigration reporting requirement (see *Travel and address reporting*).

## Emergency contacts and parents

Emergency contacts are edited on the Profile page. Adding or removing a
parent or guardian's portal access is a FERPA change made on the Enrollment →
FERPA page, not a profile edit.
