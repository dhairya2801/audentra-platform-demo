## Dropping vs withdrawing

- Through the **add/drop deadline** (11 September 2026 for Fall; 29 January
  2027 for Spring) a course can be dropped in the portal with no record and
  no charge.
- After it, leaving a course is a **withdrawal**: the course stays on the
  transcript with a **W**, which does not affect the GPA but counts as an
  attempted, not completed, course for satisfactory academic progress.
- The last day to withdraw from a course is **6 November 2026** (Fall) and
  **2 April 2027** (Spring). After that date a student remains enrolled and
  receives the earned grade, unless a medical withdrawal is approved.

Course withdrawals are submitted on the Classrooms page and take effect the
day they are submitted. A withdrawal that would take a student below 12
credits triggers the checks in *Course load and overload*.

## Withdrawing from the university

A student who leaves Aster entirely during a term submits a **university
withdrawal** to the Registrar (portal form). The effective date is the date
the form is received, and it drives the tuition refund:

| Withdrawal received (Fall 2026) | Tuition and fees refunded |
| --- | --- |
| Through 11 September 2026 (add/drop) | 100% |
| 12–18 September 2026 | 75% |
| 19–25 September 2026 | 50% |
| 26 September – 2 October 2026 | 25% |
| From 3 October 2026 | none |

Spring 2027 uses the same steps from the add/drop deadline (29 January 2027):
75% through 5 February, 50% through 12 February, 25% through 19 February.

The enrollment deposit, the orientation fee and the health-insurance charge
after the waiver deadline are not refundable. Housing and meal-plan charges
are prorated under the *Housing contract and cancellation* policy. Financial
aid that has disbursed is returned to its source in proportion to the
unearned part of the term (the federal Return of Title IV calculation); this
can leave a balance owed, which Student Accounts bills after the withdrawal
is processed.

## Medical and compassionate withdrawal

A withdrawal after the course-withdrawal deadline, or a refund beyond the
schedule, is possible for a documented serious medical condition or family
emergency. Requests go to the Registrar with documentation within 30 days of
the last day of attendance; the University Registrar decides with the
Director of Student Health or Counseling, normally within ten business days.
An approved medical withdrawal records W grades for every course and refunds
tuition on a prorated basis to the last day of attendance.

## International students

An F-1 or J-1 student must meet a DSO **before** withdrawing; a withdrawal
ends the student's immigration status and starts a 15-day grace period to
leave the country, unless an authorised early withdrawal is recorded first.
