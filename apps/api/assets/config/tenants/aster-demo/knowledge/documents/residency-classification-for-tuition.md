## The classification

Every domestic student is classified as **in-state** or **out-of-state**
for tuition at admission, from the application. In-state tuition for
2026–2027 is $18,400; out-of-state is $28,900. International students on a
visa are classified as international ($31,200) and are not eligible for
in-state classification.

## In-state criteria

In-state classification requires that the student — or, for a dependent
student, a parent or legal guardian — has been **domiciled in the state for
at least twelve consecutive months** immediately before the first day of
classes, with intent to remain: evidence includes a state driver's licence or
identity card, voter registration, a lease or deed, state income-tax filing
and employment. Presence in the state primarily to attend school does not
establish domicile. In-state students submit the **in-state residency
affidavit** through the Documents page when asked; the Registrar reviews it
within five business days.

Exceptions to the twelve-month rule: active-duty military and their
dependents stationed in the state; recipients of the State Access Grant;
graduates of a state high school who attended it for at least three years.

## Reclassification

A student classified out-of-state who later meets the criteria petitions the
Registrar with documentation. Petitions filed by the **add/drop deadline**
apply to the current term; later petitions apply from the next term.
Decisions take up to ten business days and may be appealed to the University
Registrar within ten business days.

## Effect on aid

A change of classification changes the cost of attendance; the Office of
Financial Aid re-packages the student's aid after the Registrar's decision.
