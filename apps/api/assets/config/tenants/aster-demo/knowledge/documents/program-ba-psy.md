## Overview

The Bachelor of Arts in Psychology (**Department of Social Sciences**, **120
credits**) is a research-methods-centred degree; students complete a
capstone in a faculty laboratory or an applied placement.

## Major requirements

| Course | Credits | Recommended term | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PSY 101 Introduction to Psychology | 3 | 1 | — |
| STAT 101 Introduction to Statistics | 3 | 2 | — |
| PSY 210 Research Methods | 3 | 3 | PSY 101, STAT 101 |
| PSY 320 Cognitive Psychology | 3 | 4 | PSY 210 |
| PSY 330 Developmental Psychology | 3 | 4 | PSY 101 |
| PSY 340 Social Psychology, PSY 350 Abnormal Psychology | 6 | 5 | PSY 210 |
| BIO 101 General Biology (also the core Laboratory Science) | 4 | 2 | — |
| Psychology electives | 12 | 5–7 | PSY 210 |
| PSY 490 Capstone | 3 | 8 | PSY 320 or PSY 340 |

Grade of C or better in every PSY course.

## First-year plan

Fall: PSY 101, MATH 120, WRIT 101, AST 100, one humanities course (13
credits). Spring: STAT 101, BIO 101, one social-science course, one arts
course, one elective (16 credits).

## Entry

Open admission.
