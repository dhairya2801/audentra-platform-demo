## Recording an absence

Leave, vacation, sick days, conferences and training are recorded on the
staff member's calendar (time off) with whether bookings are blocked.
Recording is what makes the portal show the student the adviser's status
and offer the covering calendar; an absence not recorded looks like an
adviser ignoring appointments.

## Leave (parental, medical, sabbatical)

- The **Assistant Director, First-Year Advising** covers first-year
  advisees; the **Assistant Director, Transfer & Spring Admits** covers
  transfer and Spring-admit advisees, for the length of the leave. The
  student's record keeps the original adviser with *on leave until (date)*.
- Appointments already booked with the adviser move to the covering
  assistant director's calendar; the student is notified by portal message.
- Work items owned by the adviser escalate to the Director on the day the
  leave is recorded and are reassigned to the covering assistant director.
- Returning from leave, the adviser resumes the caseload; no reassignment.

## Departure

- Mark the staff record *departed* with the end date; block bookings from
  that date.
- Reassign the whole caseload **within ten business days** by department
  match, respecting caps; students with an open urgent item are reassigned
  first. Until reassigned, coverage is as for leave.
- Open work items and unclosed appointments (no outcome recorded) are
  reassigned by the Director the same week.

## Caps and capacity

Caseload caps are 110–160 depending on role (Faculty Advisers 110; Senior
Academic Advisers 160). The Director reviews weekly: advisers **over cap**
receive no new assignments until below cap; advisers with **no open slots
in the next 14 days** open overflow hours or the department's other advisers
take their walk-ins; advisers with **spare capacity** (below 70% of cap)
take the unassigned pool first. Accepted students with no adviser five
business days after deposit posting are assigned by the Director from the
Morning Brew's list.

## New hires

A new adviser's first week is recorded as *training* with bookings blocked;
students are not assigned until the second week.
