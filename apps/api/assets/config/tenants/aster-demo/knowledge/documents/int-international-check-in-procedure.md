## Before the window

Two weeks before check-in, run the list of international students with an
active I-20 and no check-in appointment; message each with the window and
what to bring. Students who report they cannot arrive by the program start
date are deferred (once, up to 30 days, with the academic department's
confirmation) or reissued an I-20 for the next term; record the decision on
the student's international requirement.

## At check-in

Verify: passport valid six months; visa class matches the I-20/DS-2019;
I-20 signed by the student; I-94 shows F-1 (or J-1) and D/S; SEVIS fee
receipt; local address recorded on the profile. Capture copies into the
document record (category *identity*). Confirm the health-plan enrollment,
schedule the TB test where the questionnaire indicates, and direct
conditional admits to the English placement assessment.

## After check-in

Register the student in SEVIS within **two business days**; mark the
check-in appointment *completed* with an outcome note; the registration
gate clears automatically. Students with a transfer-in record: confirm the
release date and issue the transfer I-20 within five business days.

## No-shows

A student who has not checked in by the program start date and has not
contacted the office is messaged and called; with no response by the 30-day
deferral limit, the record is set to *no show* in SEVIS and the offer is
referred to Admissions for a Spring I-20. Every step is logged on the work
item.
