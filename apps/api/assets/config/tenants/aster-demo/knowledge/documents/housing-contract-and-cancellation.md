## Term of the contract

Signing the housing application creates a **contract for the full academic
year** (both Fall and Spring, or Spring only for Spring entrants). The
contract covers the room assigned and the meal plan chosen, billed half each
term with tuition.

## Cancelling before the year begins (Fall 2026 contracts)

| Cancellation received | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| On or before 30 June 2026 | none |
| 1 July – 15 August 2026 | $250 |
| 16 August 2026 onward, before moving in | $500 |

Cancellation is submitted on the Housing page. First-year students may cancel
only with an approved residency exemption; a first-year cancellation without
one is not accepted. A student who withdraws from Aster before the term or
defers with Admissions' approval pays no fee.

## Release during the year

A student who has moved in may be released from the contract only for:
withdrawal or leave of absence from the university; approved study away or
an internship more than 50 miles from campus; graduation in December; or an
exemption granted under the *First-year residency requirement* for a change
in circumstances. Requests go to Housing & Residence Life with documentation
and are decided within five business days. A release charges the room and
meal plan **prorated to the check-out date** plus a $500 fee, except for
withdrawal or graduation (no fee). Moving out without a release does not end
the charges.

## Spring cancellation by continuing residents

A resident who wishes to leave housing for Spring 2027 without one of the
grounds above may apply for a **buy-out**: $500 and the Fall charges, with
the Spring charges cancelled, decided by 4 December 2026. Buy-outs are not
available to first-year students.

## Refunds

Prepayments and prorated charges are credited to the student account and
refunded under *Refunds and credit balances*.
