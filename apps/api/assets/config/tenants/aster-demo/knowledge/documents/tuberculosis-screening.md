## Who is screened

All **F-1 and J-1 students** and all **Nursing students** complete the TB
risk questionnaire on the immunization checklist item. Students who lived
for more than one month in a country with a high incidence of tuberculosis,
had contact with active TB, or have symptoms need a test.

## The test

An **IGRA blood test** (preferred) or a tuberculin skin test, performed
within **six months before arrival** or at Student Health Services during
International Check-In week (21–22 August 2026; plan members free). Results
from abroad are accepted if from a laboratory and dated.

## Results

- Negative: the checklist item can be accepted once the vaccines are in
  order.
- Positive: a chest X-ray within 30 days (Student Health arranges it) and a
  clinical evaluation. A student with no active disease is cleared; latent
  TB treatment is offered but not required. Active disease is treated under
  public-health rules before the student attends classes in person; remote
  attendance is arranged without academic penalty.

Nursing students repeat screening annually before clinical placement.
