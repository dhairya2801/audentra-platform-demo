## The requirement

Applicants whose secondary schooling was not conducted in English submit one
accepted score report before the offer becomes unconditional: TOEFL iBT 80
(no section below 18), IELTS Academic 6.5 (no band below 6.0), Duolingo
English Test 110, or Cambridge C1 Advanced 176. Scores must be from within
two years of the first day of classes and sent directly by the testing
service to Admissions.

Exempt: applicants who completed at least three years of secondary school or
one year of full-time post-secondary study taught in English, and citizens of
countries on the Admissions exemption list published with the application.

## Conditional admission without a score

An applicant admitted without an accepted score is a **conditional admit** on
the English condition. The condition clears when a score arrives, or when the
student passes the **placement assessment** given during International
Check-In (21–22 August 2026). A student who does not pass enrols in WRIT 099
Academic English (non-credit, no tuition beyond the standard full-time rate)
alongside a reduced set of courses in the first term; the reduced load is
authorised by a DSO and does not affect immigration status.

## Who to contact

Score submission and exemptions: Office of Admissions. Placement assessment
and the reduced-load authorisation: International Student Services.
