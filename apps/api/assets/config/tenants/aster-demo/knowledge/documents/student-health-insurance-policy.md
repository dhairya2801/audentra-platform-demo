## The charge

Students enrolled at least half-time are enrolled in the Aster student
health plan and charged **$1,850** for the plan year (15 August 2026 – 14
August 2027) on the Fall bill; students entering in Spring are charged a
prorated $1,110.

## Waiving

A student with comparable coverage — an employer or family plan that covers
care near campus, including mental health, with an out-of-pocket maximum of
$8,000 or less — submits the waiver form with the insurance card on the
Financials page by:

- **11 September 2026** (Fall) or
- **29 January 2027** (Spring entrants).

Student Accounts confirms with the insurer and reverses the charge within
ten business days. A waiver is for the whole plan year; a student whose other
coverage ends mid-year enrols in the university plan from that date. Late
waivers are not accepted; the charge stands.

## International students

**F-1 and J-1 students cannot waive** the plan. The only exception is a
student whose government or embassy sponsor provides coverage documented on
the sponsor letter, which International Student Services must confirm.

## Using the plan

Care at Student Health Services is free to plan members; referrals and
prescriptions carry the plan's co-payments. Plan documents are on the
Financials page.
